## Slide 4: The Problem — Current Tech Stack

- **Fragmented:** 6-7 core vendors, ~20 apps across the ecosystem
- **Member management focused** — the hub exists, but it's limited
- **No control of the member experience** — tech providers are driving strategy
- **Franchisees on different websites** — no brand consistency
- **The app is informational/utility** — not experiential, not a service platform
- **Not built for national marketing ROI** — can't run unified campaigns
- **Your tech provider is your strategy** vs. having a strategy that tech supports
- *Key visual: Fragmented tech landscape diagram — scattered logos/boxes showing disconnection*

---

## Slide 5: The Assemble Strategy

- **Build** = build everything custom from scratch (maximum control, highest cost & time)
- **Buy** = purchase an off-the-shelf platform (fastest, least control, vendor lock-in)
- **Assemble** = own the core (member experience, data, middleware) and plug in best-of-breed vendors for everything else
- **Our recommendation: Assemble**
  - You own what matters most (experience + data)
  - Vendors compete on capability, not lock you in
  - Faster than full build, more control than full buy
  - Built to evolve — swap vendors without rebuilding
- *Key visual: Simple diagram showing Assemble as the middle path with its advantages*
