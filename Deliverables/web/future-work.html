CORPORATE AS SUPER-FRANCHISEE

Corporate clubs (~50 locations) go first, running on the same platform franchisees will use. This validates the system in production, builds internal expertise, creates SOPs from real operations, and demonstrates to franchisees that corporate is eating its own cooking. No special treatment, no parallel systems.
