## **Golds Gym Survey Overview \- Internal Doc**

### Recommendations: 

* Marketing the Survey   
  * Text, Email  
  * Landing Page Header Video  
* Survey   
* Data and Results

### Marketing the Survey 

Timeline: 2 Weeks for Active Survey Completion  
*(This accommodates busy schedules but sets a not-too-distant due date for the survey.)*

Start: Jan 26, 2026

End: Feb 11, 2026

Ideally Gold’s Gym compiles ‘about language’ and a brief video introduction setting the expectations for the survey and the purpose of the information. This video can be a recorded screenshare or other similar tool without overproducing the content; however, this fosters a stronger relationship with Don Murphy and the franchisees. 

Here are the recommended marketing actions for high survey engagement and completion:

* Gold’s Gym sends a text and/or email with a link to complete the survey. 

* The email and the landing page for the survey include an About Section and embedded video explaining the purpose, the timeline and the outcome of the survey. The language should also include contact information for any questions and follow up from franchisees. 

* Gold’s Gym sends a follow up text and/or email to the incomplete and not started franchisees.

* Completed surveys receive a thank you email.

### Survey

There are three surveys in order to send the correct information to the correct executives within each franchise operation. 

1. Technology  
2. Process  
3. Data and Dashboards

### Data and Results

Results will be collected in an excel sheet and added to the growing One System knowledge base. Initial analysis and key takeaways will be reviewed and compiled for Gold’s Gym executive leadership review. The raw data will be made available to product and development teams for further requirements generation.
