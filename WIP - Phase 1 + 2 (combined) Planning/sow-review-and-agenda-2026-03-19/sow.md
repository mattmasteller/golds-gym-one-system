**[APPENDIX C]{.mark}**

**Engagement Phases**

This is a high-level breakdown, with estimate, of the Engagement phases to fully develop and implement the Client Technology Stack. These timelines, work and Work Product deliverables are all good faith estimates and not final commitments and only used for general planning & discussion. The Phase 0 Audit will help the Company refine the timeline, work and Work Product into a more formal work plan. SOW No. 02 governs only Services to be provided under some of Phase 1 and Phase 2.

**Phase 0 --- Audit & Discovery (4--6 weeks) + 2 Week Contingency**\
Work: knowledge transfer, stakeholder interviews, surveys, current-state stack & data review, pain-point mapping, risk & dependency register.\
Work Product Deliverables: Current-State Map, Gap/Risk Report, Leadership Decision List.

**Phase 1 --- Decisions & Strategy (4--6 weeks)**\
Work: architectural guardrails, "system of one" principles, data-first & AI-readiness strategy, governance, franchise policies (required vs. optional), KPI tree.\
Deliverables: Gold's Gym Strategy & Playbook (signed off).

**Phase 2 --- Architecture & Blueprint (4--6 weeks)**\
Work: target architecture, data model, integration topology, identity/permissions, reporting layer, AI enablement points, implementation wave plan.\
Deliverables: Architecture Blueprint & Implementation Plan.

**Phase 3 --- Resourcing, RFPs & Vendor Selection (6-8 weeks)**\
Work: RFPs, scorecards, bake-offs/POCs, commercials, SLAs, timeline & responsibility matrix (RACI), budget envelope.\
Deliverables: Vendor Selections + Resource Plan + Master Timeline.

**Phase 4 --- Implementation Oversight (initial 16--24 weeks)**\
Work: PMO/advisory, backlog hygiene, cutover/runbooks, data migration oversight, security & compliance checks, reporting pack stand-up.\
Deliverables: Systems live for corporate clubs; onboarding-ready.

**Phase 5 --- Beta with Corporate Clubs (6 - 12 weeks)**\
Work: pilot configuration, SOP draft/iterate, training, NPS/operational metrics, fix/finish list, acceptance criteria validation.\
Deliverables: Pilot Report, Finalized SOPs, Go/No-Go.

**Phase 6 --- Franchisee Rollout & Onboarding (12--24 weeks)**\
Work: rollout playbook, enablement/training content, tiered support, data checks, required vs. opt-in system packaging/pricing guidance, governance cadence.\
Deliverables: Rollout Factory + Training Assets + Adoption Dashboards.

**Phase 7 --- Ongoing Advisory & Governance (Monthly)**\
Work: steering committee, roadmap refresh, vendor performance management, data/AI use-case incubation, franchise feedback loop. Deliverables: Monthly scorecards & roadmap updates.
