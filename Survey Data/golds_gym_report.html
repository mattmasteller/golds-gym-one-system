Strategic Insight

Franchise operators score their core systems lowest in Member Experience (3.03/5) and Reporting & Analytics (3.32/5) — the two areas most critical to growth and retention. Meanwhile, 51% are on month-to-month contracts, representing a significant window of opportunity for platform migration with minimal contractual friction.

Strategic Recommendation

The unified platform should prioritize system integration and data accuracy first (addressing #1 concern), followed by an improved member-facing app (lowest satisfaction score, most friction). A transparent, opt-in benchmarking program would unlock goodwill with the 84% of franchisees conditionally open to sharing data — creating a competitive differentiator for the entire network.
