03
Key Patterns & Strategic Insights
ABC Fitness Dominance
ABC Ignite is now confirmed as the CMS backbone for Planet Fitness, EōS Fitness, UFC Gym, and Crunch (franchise locations). Anytime Fitness uses ABC for billing/RCM. ABC is the undisputed HVLP standard.
Implication for Gold’s Gold’s is already in the ABC ecosystem. Leverage this for standardization and negotiating power. Cross-reference Planet Fitness, EōS, and UFC Gym implementations for best practices.
Crunch’s Corp/Franchise Split
Crunch runs Motionsoft (corporate) and ABC Ignite (franchise) — a dual-CMS model. CRM is Club OS (VFP) with SMG (smg360) for CX analytics. Franchise management uses FranConnect.
Implication for Gold’s Crunch’s dual-system approach demonstrates the corporate-vs-franchise technology tension Gold’s will need to navigate in its own standardization strategy.
AI Emerging as Differentiator
EōS deployed EGYM Genius AI across all clubs (first major US chain). Zenoti offers AI Receptionist, Lead Manager, and Concierge with 34% churn reduction. Both Crunch and UFC Gym are testing Enhance for PT.
Implication for Gold’s AI-powered personalization and automation are moving from “nice to have” to competitive necessity. Gold’s should evaluate AI capabilities in any platform decision.
Custom Data Warehouses Are the Norm
Planet Fitness, Crunch, and EōS all use ABC data extracts fed into custom data warehouses. Crunch adds Domo, EōS adds Power BI. No brand relies solely on their CMS vendor’s native analytics.
Implication for Gold’s Plan for a standalone data warehouse/BI layer from the start. ABC Ignite Insights alone won’t be sufficient — competitors supplement with Domo, Power BI, or custom DW solutions.
FranConnect Emerging for Franchise Ops
Both Crunch and UFC Gym use FranConnect for franchise management — a clear signal that dedicated franchise platforms are gaining traction over internal tools for multi-unit franchise operations.
Implication for Gold’s FranConnect should be evaluated for Gold’s franchise management needs. Two major competitors have already adopted it, suggesting it’s purpose-built for the fitness franchise model.
Member App = Table Stakes
All 7 competitors have proprietary branded mobile apps. Digital content is a standard differentiator — Planet Fitness (iFIT), LA Fitness (Les Mills+), 24 Hour (24GO Live), Crunch (Crunch+ via Intellivideo), UFC (Forté).
Implication for Gold’s Every major competitor has invested in member-facing digital experiences. Gold’s needs a clear mobile app and digital content strategy to remain competitive.
Transparency Gaps Are Universal
LA Fitness remains nearly opaque across the entire stack. Payment processors and access control hardware are rarely disclosed publicly. Corporate sales platforms are unknown for 5 of 7 brands.
Implication for Gold’s Gold’s should not feel behind — most competitors face the same documentation gaps. FDD exhibits and vendor case studies are the fastest path to close intel gaps.
Franchise Variability
Crunch runs different systems for corporate vs. franchise. Anytime Fitness shows tightest franchisor control (FDD-mandated vendors: Club OS + ProVision + ABC). Planet Fitness’s franchise management platform remains unknown.
Implication for Gold’s Gold’s franchise standardization strategy should study Anytime Fitness’s FDD-mandated approach as a model for vendor control.
Vendor Support Is Industry-Wide Pain
ABC Ignite: offshore support, weeks-to-months response. Zenoti: drops after onboarding. ClubReady: “absolute joke” per users. Support SLAs should be a top evaluation criterion.
Implication for Gold’s Negotiate explicit SLAs upfront. Require non-cherry-picked customer references. Budget for internal support capacity regardless of vendor promises.
