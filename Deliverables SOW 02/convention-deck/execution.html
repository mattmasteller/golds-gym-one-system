02 ORGANIZATIONAL EXPANSION

The platform requires a dedicated operating team. This is not a massive engineering buildout — it's a focused team that manages the Assemble model.

DATA TEAM
  • Data architecture and governance
  • BI and reporting infrastructure
  • Analytics and insights
  • AI/ML pipeline management
INTEGRATION TEAM
  • API development and management
  • Vendor integration and onboarding
  • Platform reliability and monitoring
  • Security and compliance
PRODUCT TEAM
  • Website and app product management
  • Consumer experience design
  • Feature prioritization and roadmap
  • User research and testing
DIGITAL OPERATIONS
  • Franchisee support and onboarding
  • Vendor relationship management
  • Training and documentation
  • Platform operations and SLA management
ASSET-LIGHT STRUCTURE

This is not a 50-person engineering department. It's a focused team that manages vendors, owns the integration layer, and operates the platform. Corporate-owned locations are treated like franchisees — they use the same platform, same processes, same tools. The Assemble model keeps the team lean because vendors handle their own infrastructure and development.

03 VENDOR SOP & PARTICIPATION

Every vendor that participates in the Gold's Gym platform must meet a consistent set of standards. No exceptions.

RequirementStandardPurpose
Onboarding Standard integration playbook with defined milestones Consistent, predictable vendor integration
API Integration RESTful APIs conforming to Gold's data model Seamless data flow into central platform
Data Ownership All data belongs to Gold's; vendor has usage rights only Protect member data and prevent vendor lock-in
Performance SLA with uptime, response time, and support requirements Reliable platform experience for franchisees
Compliance Alignment with Gold's security, privacy, and brand policies Risk management and regulatory compliance
Exit Strategy Data export and migration plan required in every contract Ability to swap vendors without losing data or continuity

All vendors operate within our system, not outside of it. This governance framework is not optional. It's the mechanism that makes the Assemble model work at scale. Vendors that can't meet these standards don't participate.

04 VENDOR RFP EVALUATION RUBRIC

A standardized scoring framework applied consistently across all vendor conversations. Every vendor is evaluated against the same criteria to ensure objective, apples-to-apples comparison.

CriteriaWeightWhat We're Evaluating
API & Integration Quality 25% RESTful API maturity, webhook support, real-time data sync, documentation quality, sandbox availability
Data Ownership & Portability 20% Data export capabilities, migration support, contractual data ownership terms, no vendor lock-in
Franchise Support Model 15% Multi-location architecture, corporate vs. franchisee controls, onboarding at scale, training resources
Core Feature Completeness 15% Member lifecycle coverage (join, billing, check-in, freeze, cancel), POS, scheduling, reporting
Commercial Terms 10% Pricing model, volume discounts, migration incentives, co-marketing funds, revenue sharing
Platform Reliability & SLA 10% Uptime guarantees, support response times, incident management, scalability track record
Innovation & Roadmap 5% AI/ML capabilities, mobile-first design, product velocity, willingness to co-develop
NON-NEGOTIABLES
  • All member data owned by Gold's — not the vendor
  • Full API access with real-time sync capability
  • Multi-location franchise architecture (not bolted-on)
  • Contractual data export and migration plan
  • U.S. market coverage and support
MEETING SHORT LIST
  • ABC — incumbent, upgraded platform evaluation
  • Zenoti — modern franchise-native architecture
  • Perfect Gym (Sports Alliance) — global scale, API-first
  • Exerp — enterprise fitness management
  • Hapana — modern fitness platform
  • Fitness Force — international consideration

COMMERCIAL OPPORTUNITIES
  • Volume leverage — 700+ locations creates significant purchasing power
  • Vendor funding — migration incentives, implementation support, co-marketing
  • Competitive bids — RFP process drives better pricing and terms
  • Partnership models — revenue sharing, co-development, and strategic alignment
RISK FACTORS
  • Vendor lock-in — mitigated by data ownership and exit clauses
  • Migration complexity — managed through phased rollout
  • Contract timing — existing agreements may constrain transition windows
  • Franchisee adoption — earned through better experience, not mandates

Commercial strategy is as important as technical strategy. Gold's has significant leverage with 700+ locations. The RFP process will surface the best options, the best pricing, and the best partnership models. This is a buyers' market.
