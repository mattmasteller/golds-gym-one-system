02 NON-NEGOTIABLES

If a vendor cannot meet these requirements, they do not advance past initial evaluation. No exceptions.

MUST HAVE
  • Data ownership — All member data belongs to Gold's, not the vendor. Full contractual data ownership.
  • Full API access — RESTful APIs with real-time sync capability, not batch exports.
  • Multi-location franchise architecture — Built for franchise from the ground up, not bolted onto a single-club product.
  • Data export & migration plan — Contractual commitment to full data portability and migration support.
  • U.S. market support — Domestic coverage, support hours, and compliance.
  • POS integration — Retail point-of-sale capabilities integrated into or tightly coupled with the member management system.
STRATEGIC FIT
  • Assemble model compatibility — Vendor must plug into Gold's integration layer, not require Gold's to adopt their ecosystem.
  • Centralized data, decentralized execution — Corporate sees everything; franchisees control their locations.
  • No consumer-facing apps — All member-facing experiences go through Gold's platform (website, app), not the vendor's.
  • Brand standard compliance — Vendor UX conforms to Gold's brand guidelines where member-visible.
  • Willingness to co-develop — Open to partnership model, not take-it-or-leave-it SaaS.

04 KEY QUESTIONS FOR EVERY VENDOR

Ask these in every meeting. Consistent questions produce comparable answers.

ARCHITECTURE & INTEGRATION
  1. Walk us through your API architecture. REST? GraphQL? Webhooks?
  2. Do you have a sandbox or staging environment for integration testing?
  3. How do you handle real-time data sync vs. batch?
  4. What does your onboarding/integration timeline look like for a 700+ location network?
  5. Can your system feed data into a central data lake (e.g., Databricks, Snowflake)?
FRANCHISE MODEL
  1. How is your platform architected for multi-location franchise operations?
  2. What controls does corporate have vs. individual franchisees?
  3. How do you handle franchisee onboarding at scale?
  4. Can corporate enforce standardized configurations while allowing local flexibility?
  5. What's your largest franchise deployment today? Lessons learned?
DATA & COMMERCIAL
  1. Who owns the data contractually? What happens when we leave?
  2. What data export formats do you support? Full member history?
  3. What is your pricing model? Per-location? Per-member? Per-transaction?
  4. Do you offer migration incentives or implementation funding?
  5. What does your SLA look like? Uptime commitment? Support tiers?
PRODUCT & ROADMAP
  1. What's on your 12-month product roadmap?
  2. How do you handle POS / retail transactions within the platform?
  3. What AI/ML or personalization capabilities do you have or plan?
  4. Are you open to co-development or custom feature work?
  5. How do you handle the join-online flow? Can we own the front-end UX?
CONTEXT FOR VENDORS

Gold's Gym is pursuing an Assemble architecture — Gold's owns the data layer, consumer experience (website, app), and integration layer. Best-in-class vendors plug into our platform via APIs. We are not looking for a vendor to own our consumer experience. We are looking for a member management engine that operates within our ecosystem, conforms to our data standards, and scales across 700+ locations.

Current state: ABC / Motionsoft — scored 2.2/5 in Phase 0 assessment. This is a competitive RFP process with the goal of vendor selection by August 2026.
