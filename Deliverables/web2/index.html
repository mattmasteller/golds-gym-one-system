03 BUILD v ASSEMBLE v BUY

Three paths exist for Gold's Gym to solve its technology challenges. Each carries distinct tradeoffs in cost, control, speed, and long-term ownership.

BUILD
  • Custom-built platform tailored to Gold's exact needs
  • Full control over roadmap and features
  • Highest cost and longest timeline
  • Requires large dedicated engineering team
  • Ongoing maintenance burden is permanent
Not the best fit for Gold's current priorities and timeline
ASSEMBLE
  • Best-in-class components integrated via APIs
  • Retain Motion Vibe, add core gym platform via RFP
  • Own the integration layer and data
  • Moderate cost, moderate timeline to deployment
  • Small team manages vendor relationships
Matches Gold's DNA
BUY
  • Single-vendor suite for all functionality
  • Fastest deployment
  • Locked into vendor's roadmap and limitations
  • No vendor covers all Gold's requirements today
  • Migration risk concentrated in one partner
No single vendor fits

The message is clear: Gold's must own its data and integration layer while leveraging best-in-class vendors for each functional area. This is the "Assemble" model — and it's exactly how every successful fitness franchisor operates.

04 OUR RECOMMENDATION
THE ASSEMBLE MODEL

Gold's Gym should assemble a best-in-class technology platform by selecting a core gym management system through a competitive RFP, retaining Motion Vibe for group exercise, adding specialized components for CRM, CDP, and analytics, and building an integration layer that Gold's owns and controls.

This approach matches Gold's asset-light DNA, mirrors how every successful fitness franchisor operates, and keeps Gold's in control of its data and member relationships.
