# Gold's Gym Executive Discovery Session

**Dates:** March 31 - April 1, 2026
**Company:** StackHack Labs
**Total Duration:** ~12 hours (4h Day 1 + 8h Day 2)

---

## 3 Categories of Discovery

1. **Strategic Questions** — Vision, business model, technology direction
2. **Personas & Member Journeys** — Who are the users? What are their experiences?
3. **Consumer Experience Tech | Vendors** — Current stack, gaps, build vs. buy

**Flow:** Strategy sets the foundation. Personas emerge from strategy. Journeys bring personas to life. Vendor decisions follow from journey gaps. _Never reverse this order._

### Block 2: Member Persona Identification (3:30 - 5:00, ~1.5 hrs)

Using the strategic direction from Block 1, identify and define the member personas that matter for Gold's Gym.

**Format:** Facilitator brings reference personas from `user-story-mapping.md` as prompts, but the room defines what's real for Gold's.

#### Activities

1. **Brainstorm** all member types that will interact with the Gold's brand (15 min)
2. **Cluster and name** primary personas — expect 4-8 (20 min)
3. **Define each persona** — goals, frustrations, primary channels, lifecycle stage (30 min)
4. **Flag personas** that need full journey mapping on Day 2 (15 min)
5. **Wrap-up** — confirm persona set, note any open questions (10 min)

#### Reference Personas (starting point, not prescriptive)

These come from the platform's user-story-mapping exercise. The room may combine, split, rename, or add to these:

- Prospective Member (researching, comparing, joining)
- Active Member (daily gym use, classes, training)
- At-Risk / Lapsed Member (declining engagement, reactivation)
- Family Account Holder (dependents, Kids Club, family billing)
- Guest / Day Pass User (trial visits, referred by member)

#### Day 1 Deliverable

Validated strategic direction + named member persona set ready for journey mapping on Day 2.

## Day 2 — Full Day (April 1, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM)

### Block 3: Member Journey Mapping (9:00 - 12:30, ~3.5 hrs)

The bulk of Day 2. Map high-level journeys for each persona identified on Day 1. This block is expected to produce **6+ journeys** — the specific journeys emerge from Day 1 strategy and personas, but examples of what may surface:

| Example Journey | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| Discover & Join | Prospect hears about Gold's → researches → tours → signs up |
| Daily Gym Visit | Member arrives → checks in → works out → leaves |
| Buy Something | eComm purchases, in-gym retail, PT packages, add-on services |
| Use Premium Amenities | Recovery room, group fitness classes, Kids Club, pool |
| Get Help / Resolve an Issue | Member has a problem → contacts support → resolution |
| I'm Losing Interest | Declining visits → outreach triggers → AI/human intervention → decision |
| Manage My Account | Billing, freeze, cancel, upgrade, payment updates |
| Family Experience | Add dependents, Kids Club booking, family billing, check-in |

#### For Each Journey, Capture:

- **Key touchpoints** — what happens at each step?
- **Channels** — app, web, in-person, AI agent, phone, SMS?
- **Pain points** — where does friction exist today?
- **Opportunities** — where can AI/automation add value?
- **Open questions** — what do we need to validate with real users?

## Facilitator Prep

### Reference Materials

- **`user-story-mapping.md`** — Full persona inventory (15 personas), user story inventory by persona (built/roadmapped/prognosticated status), recommended discovery session structure, key questions per session type, mobile app feature tiers, and backend readiness matrix. Use as a reference checklist during Blocks 2-4, not as a script.

### Pre-Session Checklist

- [ ] Print or display reference personas from `user-story-mapping.md` for Block 2
- [ ] Prepare blank journey mapping templates (physical or digital whiteboard)
- [ ] Have sticky notes / cards ready for journey touchpoint capture
- [ ] Prepare timer for keeping blocks on schedule
- [ ] Bring copies of the 9 strategic questions for Block 1

### Key Facilitation Notes

- **Block 1:** Let conversation flow naturally within each theme. The 9 questions are starting points, not a rigid script. If a question sparks deeper discussion, follow it.
- **Block 2:** Bring the reference personas as prompts but let the room define what's real for Gold's. They may combine, split, or rename personas. The goal is _their_ persona set, not ours.
- **Block 3:** This is the largest block for a reason — don't rush it. If a journey opens up unexpected territory, explore it. Better to deeply map 6 journeys than superficially cover 10.
- **Block 4:** Keep franchisee journeys high-level. These are executive-level paths, not system-level user stories.
- **Block 5:** Vendor discussion should reference specific journey gaps identified in Blocks 3-4. Avoid abstract vendor evaluation.
- **Block 6:** Resist the urge to prioritize or scope. The goal is clean capture and clear next steps, not decisions about what to build first.
