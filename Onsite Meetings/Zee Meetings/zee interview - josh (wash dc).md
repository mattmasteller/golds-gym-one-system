## Key Insights & Perspectives

### On ABC Financial

**Strong Advocate:**
> "I don't know how another company would be considered other than ABC because of how many franchisees are already on it currently and the risk to mitigate people off that to another, maybe new company."

**Reliability:**
> "When people call me and they say, hey, but ABC doesn't work. And I go at them and I say, yeah, but what doesn't work? And then they say, oh, well, it's Gym Sales that doesn't work. And I'm like, well, that's the CRM. Yes, it's part of ABC, but I'm talking about the operating platform."

**Key Questions for Critics:**
- "Does your Friday, does your 15th, 25th, does your billing go through? Every time."
- "Do you ever have an issue with that?"
- "When you call them to service something, are they responsive?"

**On Competitors:**
> "New companies can show you flashy, cool, good looking things because they're in sales mode... sometimes not even their real product, right? It's still in their MVP and they're like, look what we can do. And then six months later, they still can't do it."

### On Platform Transitions

**Motion Soft to ABC Experience:**
> "When we switched to ABC, we found revenue. Because they have such a diligent conversion process because they've done it so many times."

**Motion Soft Problems:**
- Lack of flexibility
- Couldn't scale
- Missing billing runs ("if we bill on a Friday, sometimes I would find that we didn't bill clubs")
- Money not being reinvested after acquisition

**Transition Timeline:**
- Active process: 60-90 days
- Total timeline including prep: ~6 months

### On Website Issues

**Why Own Website:**
> "In order for us to move as fast as we wanted, we had to do our own thing. Whether it was tracking, functionality, SEO."

**Corporate Site Problems:**
> "The lack of flexibility, the lack of tracking, the lack of info. We just had to do it."

**Philosophical Difference:**
> "It's hard to get all franchisees and corporate on the same page on what they want a website to do... For us, the website is your sales tool... we need to sell memberships and get leads."

### On the Gold's App

**ABC App Choice:**
> "For me it was about what's going to be a simplistic approach with non-friction for the members. If we're on ABC, let's have the app be powered by ABC."

**Key Features:**
- Working barcode
- Geofence recognition (barcode pops up when arriving)
- Billing changes
- Workout tracking

**On Motion Vibe:**
> "I get it. Some of these people can show these prezos with their, sometimes not even their real product."

## Fitness BI / GymStats Deep Dive

### Dashboard Capabilities (Demonstrated in Interview)
- Sales budget variance percentage
- Actual MTD vs. prior year
- Budget comparisons
- Percent variance and dollar variance
- Club ranking reports
- Daily weighting (sales weighted by day of week)

### Daily Weighting System
- Monday weighted higher than Sunday
- Shows what's needed to hit budget for rest of month by day
- Manually input monthly based on seasonality

### Other Metrics Tracked
- Total members
- Top 5 clubs by joins
- Active members
- EFT members
- Dues average
- Cancels

### Philosophy:
> "The whole goal is to not have to go into a system. You want something that you think is valuable to your business, click here."

### Data Normalization Warning:
> "People, what they expect something to feed into... definitions have to be exact or it's not going to necessarily work. Like, they'll be like, oh, the freaking data never matches up. Well, if it doesn't match up with ABC, it's because of a definition."
