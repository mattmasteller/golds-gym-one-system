## Gap Details

### GAP 1: Franchisee Surveys — Missing

**SOW Reference:** Appendix A, Category iii — "Franchisee Surveys (3 Areas) — Support Development; Tech, Business Process, KPI's"

No folder or documents exist. The SOW calls for surveys across 3 areas (Tech, Business Process, KPIs) targeting franchisees. This is an entire workstream with zero deliverables.

**Required:**
- Survey design and distribution across 3 areas
- Franchisee response data and analysis
- Summary of findings

---

### GAP 2: Business Process & System Audit — Missing

**SOW Reference:** Appendix A, Category vi — "Review Org Chart, Roles, Identify Gaps and Workflows"

No folder or documents exist.

**Required:**
- Org chart review and analysis
- Role descriptions and gap identification
- Workflow and process maps
- Business process gap analysis (distinct from technology gaps)

---

### GAP 3: Dashboard / KPI — Missing

**SOW Reference:** Appendix A, Category vii — "Look at Dashboard/KPI's and Define Strategic Questions"

No folder or documents exist.

**Required:**
- Current dashboard and reporting landscape review
- KPI definition and hierarchy
- Strategic questions framework for executive data needs

---

### GAP 4: Compliance and Risk Analysis — Missing Standalone Deliverable

**SOW Reference:** Appendix A, Category ix — "Data Privacy, Risk Review and Outstanding Compliance"

Risk factors are scattered across the Summary and Roadmap documents, but there is no dedicated compliance and risk analysis.

**Required:**
- Data privacy review
- Risk register with severity and mitigation
- Outstanding compliance issues and remediation plan

---

### GAP 5: Leadership Decision List — Missing

**SOW Reference:** Appendix C, Phase 0 Deliverables — "Current-State Map, Gap/Risk Report, Leadership Decision List"

No document exists. This should be a prioritized list of key decisions leadership needs to make coming out of the audit (e.g., platform selection approach, franchise migration strategy, data normalization timeline).

**Required:**
- Prioritized decision items with context
- Options and trade-offs for each decision
- Recommended path forward per item

---

### GAP 6: Current-State Map — Partially Addressed

**SOW Reference:** Appendix C, Phase 0 Deliverables — "Current-State Map"

The Current System Assessment (`04/01`) covers scoring and pain points but lacks a comprehensive current-state architecture map showing how all systems interconnect. The `environment-bubbles.png` exists but is not formalized as a deliverable.

**Required:**
- Formal current-state system architecture diagram
- Data flow mapping between systems
- Integration inventory (what connects to what, how)

---

### GAP 7: Franchisor Engagement Documentation — Thin

**SOW Reference:** Appendix A, Category viii — "Interviews, Ecosystem, Corp vs Franchisee, Operations"

Franchising (Kevin/Katie) appears in the stakeholder interviews, but there is no dedicated analysis of the franchisor/franchisee ecosystem dynamics.

**Required:**
- Corp vs. franchisee operational model analysis
- Franchise ecosystem dynamics documentation
- Engagement strategy for franchise adoption

---

### GAP 8: The "Deck" — Format Gap

**SOW Reference:** Section 4 — "The Audit and Discovery report (the 'Deck')"

All current deliverables are markdown files. The SOW explicitly calls for a presentation of findings, recommendations, and key decisions to the Client.

**Required:**
- Compiled presentation deck with all findings
- Executive summary and recommendations
- Key decisions and next steps
