## 1. About Gold's Gym & This Initiative

### 1.1 About Gold's Gym

Gold's Gym operates a network of approximately **400 locations across the United States**, under a mix of **corporate-owned and franchised** ownership. We operate an asset-light model and are actively growing the franchise base. Our member base and transaction volumes are significant and will be shared with short-listed vendors. [Gold's to confirm — member base, annual transaction volume, peak concurrent load.]

### 1.2 The Initiative — One Platform, One Experience, One Data Layer

Gold's is unifying a fragmented technology environment into a single, coherent platform. Rather than adopting one vendor's end-to-end suite, we are **assembling** a platform: **Gold's owns the data layer and the member experience (our website and app), and best-in-class vendors integrate into a Gold's-controlled integration layer via APIs.**

The Member Management System (MMS) is the operational system of record for member data, billing, check-in, and collections. It is the foundational component of this platform and the focus of this RFI.

### 1.3 What Gold's Believes (Operating Principles)

These principles define how we work with technology partners. We share them upfront so you can assess fit early:

- **"We assemble the platform — we do not rent it."**
- **"All member data belongs to Gold's, not the vendor."**
- **"Vendors operate within our system, not outside of it."**

### 1.4 Why Now

Modernizing the MMS unblocks the rest of the platform: a unified member view across locations, real-time data into our analytics layer, consistent member experience across web and app, and accurate franchise-level reporting. We are engaging vendors now to inform our path forward.

## 2. Purpose & Scope of This RFI

### 2.1 In Scope

This RFI focuses on a Member Management System with a tightly-coupled retail Point of Sale:

- Member lifecycle (join, onboarding, freeze, cancel, win-back)
- Membership plans and pricing configuration
- Billing and collections (ACH, card, dunning, chargebacks, settlement)
- Check-in and gate/kiosk integration
- Travel pass / multi-location usage tracking
- Retail POS with member-account linkage
- Operational reporting and franchisee scorecards
- Transactional member communications (receipts, billing notices, freeze confirmations)

### 2.2 Adjacent Capabilities We Are Also Gathering Information On

To understand your full platform and how it would integrate with ours, we also ask you to describe your capabilities — and your openness to third-party alternatives — in:

- **CRM** (Section 5.9)
- **Member mobile app** (Section 5.10)
- **Analytics and data access** (Section 6.3)

We are not necessarily procuring these from the MMS vendor; we want to understand whether they are native, optional, or replaceable.

### 2.3 Integration Dependencies

The MMS must integrate cleanly with — but does not need to own — our CRM, member app, payment processor, gate-control hardware, and our data warehouse. Please describe your integration capabilities throughout your response (see Sections 6.1–6.4).

## 4. Threshold Requirements

The following are baseline requirements for the platform. For each, please **confirm whether you meet it and describe how.** These reflect the operating principles in Section 1.3 and are central to our evaluation. (A confirmation checklist is provided in Appendix C.)

- **4.1 Full data ownership.** Gold's owns all member data, schema, derived data, and outputs. The vendor holds usage rights for service delivery only. Confirm this can be contractual.
- **4.2 Real-time API access.** REST APIs and webhooks supporting event-driven, real-time synchronization. Describe your API surface. Batch-only architectures do not meet this requirement.
- **4.3 Multi-location franchise architecture.** Built for franchise/multi-location from the ground up, not a single-club product with multi-location added on. Describe your data model.
- **4.4 Documented data export and migration.** Full historical export in standard formats (CSV, JSON, Parquet) on demand, and contractual cooperation with any future migration. Describe what you provide.
- **4.5 U.S. market support.** Coverage, regulatory familiarity, and support hours aligned to U.S. operations.
- **4.6 POS / retail capability.** Retail POS integrated into or tightly coupled with the MMS, so members and retail transactions share one account of truth.
- **4.7 Open ecosystem and headless compatibility.** Your platform operates as an open platform that Gold's builds on top of, not a closed suite that requires adopting your full product family for full value. APIs are first-class. Your UI is optional rather than mandatory. Gold's can integrate third-party CRM, app, marketing, analytics, and PT tools without commercial penalty, support degradation, or roadmap deprioritization. In short: you should be comfortable serving as the **headless system of record** while Gold's owns workflows and the member experience on top.

### 5.10 Member Mobile App

Describe your member mobile app capabilities, if any:

## 6. Technical, API & Integration

### 6.1 API Architecture

Describe your API architecture: REST baseline, webhooks for event-driven sync, GraphQL (if any), rate-limit documentation, versioning policy, and the availability of a sandbox/staging environment for integration testing. Please include example payloads where helpful.

### 6.2 Data Ownership & Portability

Describe how Gold's data ownership is handled contractually; export formats (CSV, JSON, Parquet) and on-demand availability; full historical export including custom attributes; and your commitment to assist migration to a successor system at termination.

### 6.3 Analytics & Data Access

This is a priority area. Please address each point:

- **Data movement.** How is data made available to Gold's — **data push, access to data feeds/streams, or a feed into our unified data layer**? Describe the mechanism (API, webhook, CDC, file) and latency. Is there an overnight-batch dependency for analytics-grade data?
- **Analytics: baked-in vs. third-party.** Is analytics/BI **fully baked into your platform**, or can it be served by a **third-party tool**? Describe both options if available.
- **Visualization & semantic model.** For visualization, can Gold's use a **third-party viewer over a Gold's-owned semantic model**, or is the analytics/visualization layer **fully baked in** (as in an all-in-one model)? Describe what Gold's owns vs. what stays inside your platform.
- **Modularity / future migration.** A likely path is starting with your analytics/data solution and later moving to Gold's own. How does your architecture support that — is the data/analytics layer **modular (e.g., containerized / microservices) and replaceable** without disrupting the rest of the platform? Describe what is involved.
- **Unified data layer.** We are standing up a central data warehouse (candidates include Databricks, Snowflake, and Microsoft Fabric; selection pending). Describe your ability to feed real-time event streams into such a platform, including schema documentation and change-data-capture or webhook-driven delivery.

### 6.4 Required Integrations

Describe how you integrate with each of the following, including integration type (API, webhook, file), data direction, and frequency:

- CRM (HubSpot)
- Member app (Motion Vibe)
- Gate-control hardware
- Payment processor
- Email and SMS providers
- Unified data layer / data warehouse

### 6.5 Performance

State numerical targets (not adjectives) for: API response time for join and check-in flows, supported concurrent load during peak gym hours, and mobile API latency for member-app integration.

### 6.6 Ecosystem Posture (Open vs. Closed)

These questions help us understand whether your platform is one Gold's builds on top of, or one that captures data, workflows, and UI inside its own walls. Please answer each in writing with **specific numbers, lists, or named examples** — not adjectives.

- **API as primary interface.** What percentage of platform functionality is exposed via public API? List modules with full API coverage and modules that are admin-UI-only. Where there are gaps, what is the timeline to close them?
- **Headless / system-of-record mode.** Can Gold's host the entire member-facing UI (join, account portal, freeze/cancel, payment management) and use the platform purely as a transaction engine and system of record? If yes, describe what is required and name customers operating this way. If no, explain specifically why.
- **API pricing and metering.** Are APIs included in the base subscription or metered/billed separately? Provide per-call cost at scale, rate-limit tiers, and whether higher API throughput requires a different commercial tier.
- **Webhook and event coverage.** Provide the full catalog of webhook events you fire today (member, billing, transaction, access, plan-change, communication events). Mark which require polling. State a latency target per event type.
- **Third-party integration policy.** Describe your integrations marketplace and approval model. Which categories of third-party integration are self-service, which require partnership/approval, and which are discouraged or blocked? Provide examples of customers running non-vendor CRM, app, marketing, and analytics tools.
- **Adjacent-module pricing pressure.** Does pricing, support tier, SLA, or roadmap prioritization change if Gold's chooses third-party tools in adjacent categories (CRM, member app, marketing, PT, analytics)? List every commercial or operational difference, including bundle discounts that change economics when customers adopt additional modules.
- **Customer customization without professional services.** Can Gold's admin users create custom fields, workflows, reports, and integrations without engaging your professional services? List capabilities, limits, and any premium-tier gating.
- **Vendor use of customer data.** Does customer data ever flow into shared analytics, vendor-internal benchmarking, AI/ML training, or product-development datasets? Under what terms? Can the customer opt out completely?
- **UI replaceability.** For each major member-facing surface (join, account, billing, freeze, cancel, check-in confirmation), can your UI be fully replaced by a Gold's-built UI calling your APIs? Where it cannot, explain the constraint.
- **Exit.** Describe what a Gold's exit from your platform looks like operationally — data formats provided, time to extract, your cooperation during transition, and what customer-owned customizations and integrations stay vs. leave. Has any customer of comparable scale exited in the last 24 months?
