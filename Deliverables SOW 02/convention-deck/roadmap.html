DECISION GATES
  • Gate 1: Strategy playbook approval — alignment on platform scope, priorities, and business model
  • Gate 2: Architecture sign-off — technical blueprint approved, integration requirements defined
  • Gate 3: Vendor selection — contracts signed, implementation plan approved
  • Gate 4: Pilot evaluation — corporate pilot results reviewed, go/no-go for beta
  • Gate 5: Rollout decision — beta results confirm readiness for franchise network

Clear path from strategy to execution. Every phase has defined deliverables, decision gates, and success criteria. No phase begins without sign-off on the previous one. This protects the investment and ensures alignment at every step.

02 PLATFORM TRANSFORMATION FOR GOLD'S GYM
ONE PLATFORM. ONE EXPERIENCE. ONE DATA LAYER.

This is how Gold's Gym scales into the future. A unified platform that powers the brand, enables data-driven decisions, and delivers a consumer experience worthy of Gold's legacy — across every location, every touchpoint, and every member interaction.

ONE PLATFORM
Unified technology stack used by every franchisee — consistency, scale, and intelligence across the network
ONE EXPERIENCE
Seamless consumer journey from first ad to daily member engagement — website, app, and in-club unified
ONE DATA LAYER
Single source of truth powering KPIs, personalization, AI, and real-time business intelligence
CORE PILLARS
  • Assemble strategy — own the data and consumer experience, integrate best-in-class vendors
  • Platform-first architecture — vendors plug into Gold's, not the other way around
  • Franchise-grade design — centralized data, decentralized execution
  • Revenue engine — corporate becomes a service provider, not a cost center
  • Phased execution — clear milestones, decision gates, and risk management
NEXT STEPS
  • Complete Phase 1-2 — finalize strategy playbook and architecture blueprint
  • Align stakeholders — board, investors, and franchise leadership
  • Launch RFP process — begin vendor evaluation for core platform components
  • Build the team — key hires for data, integration, and product
