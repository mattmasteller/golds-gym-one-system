01 STAKEHOLDER INTERVIEWS

December 9-10, 2025 — Two days of meetings at Gold's Gym HQ in Dallas/Irving, TX with executive leadership and department heads.

EXECUTIVE
Brad & Danny, co-CEOs
Scalable franchise playbook, asset-light support model, compelling value proposition for franchisee adoption. Franchise growth is the focus — target 16-25 new gyms per year.
"Three years from now, we want to have a much better traditional franchiser playbook... if we're gonna get real blue chip franchise candidates to come into this brand, we have to offer what would be considered table stakes."
OPERATIONS
Robbie / Kim
Promotion scheduling with future dates and auto-expiry, bulk configuration changes across locations, cancellation workflow automation, API reliability.
"Friday at 4pm fire drills" — promos require manual day-of configuration across 48 clubs by 1 person.
BILLING & FINANCE
Melody & team
Real-time visibility into franchise revenues, automated royalty calculation and ACH pull, multiple payment processor support, reconciliation tools.
"We're chasing payments via spreadsheets and have no visibility until they self-report."
MARKETING
Jake
SMS capability with compliance, attribution tracking from ad click to conversion, affiliate/referral tracking, CAC reduction tools.
"We can't send SMS — legal blocked it due to compliance gaps." CAC ranges $88-$125 vs. $60-$70 industry benchmark.
DIGITAL MARKETING
Alex & team
Automated Join Online configuration, standardized membership naming, CDP for retargeting and audience management.
"24 franchise groups backlogged because each requires 3-week manual onboarding."
FITNESS
Erin / Alexander
Motion Vibe integration (non-negotiable), remote PT session booking, InBody integration for body composition tracking.
"PTs can't book sessions unless they're physically in the gym on our network."
FRANCHISING
Kevin & Katie
Travel pass / reciprocity system, franchisee performance dashboards, brand-level controls with franchise overrides.
"We've been talking about this for four years now... If we wait two years, they're going to be like, 'We're just going to keep doing our own thing.'"
IT
Rick
API-first architecture, SSO/identity management, multi-factor authentication, integration with existing infrastructure (AWS, Meraki).
"Every system is a silo — data doesn't flow."
5 COMMON THEMES
1
"WE'RE NOT BEST IN CLASS"
Every department acknowledged Gold's lacks the technology infrastructure expected by sophisticated franchise candidates. Competitors (Crunch, Anytime, Planet) have better playbooks.
2
NEED FOR NORMALIZATION
The #1 technical requirement: standardize naming conventions, GL structures, membership configurations, and processes before any system can work at scale.
3
DON'T WANT TO BUILD A CALL CENTER
Brad was clear: asset-light model with minimal headcount growth. Technology must enable self-service and automation, not large support teams.
4
FRANCHISEES MUST SEE VALUE, NOT MANDATES
Change management will be the hardest part. Long-standing franchisees are skeptical. The new system must be so compelling they experience FOMO.
5
WEBSITE & APP AS QUICK WINS
Joint Online and the new Mobile App are near-term opportunities to build trust and demonstrate value before the full system migration.

MIKE WILLIAMS

CROSS-FRANCHISE INSIGHTS
  • ABC is the anchor — all three operators use ABC Financial and have converted acquired clubs to it. Josh: "When we switched to ABC, we found revenue."
  • Website is #1 pain point — Don: "Websites are a mess." Mike: "It's been two years of them talking about it." Josh built his own to move faster.
  • One System support is universal — despite different business models, all three agree that platform unification is necessary for brand growth
  • Diverse visions, shared pain — Don wants wellness integration, Josh wants simplicity, Mike wants corporate to act like a franchisor. All agree current state is untenable.
  • Franchisee autonomy tension — balancing standardization with operator flexibility is the core change management challenge
  • Urgency — Don: "The only thing I'm waiting for Gold's on is executing on the one system project." Mike: "It's been two years of talking."
