# Gold's Gym Executive Discovery Session

**Dates:** March 31 - April 1, 2026
**Company:** StackHack Labs
**Total Duration:** ~12 hours (4h Day 1 + 8h Day 2)

---

## 3 Categories of Discovery

1. **Strategic Questions** — Vision, business model, technology direction
2. **Personas & Member Journeys** — Who are the users? What are their experiences?
3. **Consumer Experience Tech | Vendors** — Current stack, gaps, build vs. buy

**Flow:** Strategy sets the foundation. Personas emerge from strategy. Journeys bring personas to life. Vendor decisions follow from journey gaps. _Never reverse this order._

## Day 1 — Half Day (March 31, 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM)

### Block 1: Strategic Pitch — Project Strong & Technology Vision (1:00 - 3:15, ~2h 15m)

StackHack presents the recommended strategy for Gold's Gym's future. The CEOs review, react, and provide directional yes/no decisions that set the foundation for persona identification, journey mapping, and vendor selection in the remaining sessions.

**Format:** StackHack-led presentation (~75 min) → CEO Decision Points (~45 min) → Open Discussion (~15 min)

#### Part A: The Pitch (1:00 - 2:15, ~75 min)

Presented by Mike & Matt. Slide deck walks through the full strategic recommendation.

| Time | Topic | Key Message |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 1:00 - 1:10 | **Build vs. Assemble vs. Buy** | Introduce the three approaches. Recommend Assemble as the path forward — best balance of speed, control, and cost. |
| 1:10 - 1:25 | **Project Strong — The Gold's Gym of the Future** | Present the vision: 30K sq ft strength-forward flagship. Core amenities (free weights, performance turf, recovery suite, posing room, content zones). Gold's owns the category of Strength & Human Performance. Reference the Austin location as the model. |
| 1:25 - 1:40 | **Current Tech Stack: The Problem** | Fragmented stack (6-7 vendors / ~20 apps). Member management focused but limited member experience control. Franchisees on different websites. App is utility, not experiential. Tech provider is driving strategy instead of supporting it. |
| 1:40 - 1:55 | **The 21st Century Approach: The Solution** | Data-first architecture. Own the member experience layer. Vendors plug into your middleware. All franchisees on one corporate website. One digital app for all offerings. App becomes a 24/7 service platform. Tech stack powered by AI. |
| 1:55 - 2:05 | **Tech Stack Architecture** | Bubble chart — show capability areas (not vendors). Then reveal color-coded view: what Gold's owns (consumer experience, data, middleware) vs. what goes to RFI/RFP with vendor partners. |
| 2:05 - 2:10 | **Technology Tied to Amenities** | Map each core amenity to the technology that supports it — show how the tech stack enables the member experience at the facility level. |
| 2:10 - 2:15 | **Market Context** | What leading comparable brands are doing. How this approach positions Gold's ahead of the market. |

**Objective:** Deliver a clear, compelling vision for where Gold's Gym technology and operations need to go — and show StackHack has a concrete path to get there.

#### Part B: CEO Decision Points (2:15 - 3:00, ~45 min)

These are the key decisions we need directional input on. Each is framed for a yes/no or A-vs-B response. StackHack presents its recommendation for each; CEOs confirm, adjust, or table for later.

| # | Decision | StackHack Recommendation |
|---|----------|--------------------------|
| 1 | **Amenities completeness** — Are we missing any core amenities that need to be accounted for in the technology stack? | We've mapped the Austin location model as the baseline. Confirm or flag additions. |
| 2 | **Build vs. hire for owned components** — Should Gold's hire internal resources or engage an agency to build the components you will own (member experience, data layer, middleware)? | Start with an agency partner (StackHack) for speed and expertise, with a path to bring key roles in-house as the platform matures. |
| 3 | **Revenue from the new stack** — Will this platform generate franchisee revenue streams (online join fees, tech fees, paid services, partner network)? | Yes — design the platform with revenue capture in mind from day one. Define the fee model now so it's architected in, not bolted on. |
| 4 | **Team structure & franchisee support** — Will the current team support only corporate locations, or also franchisee services? Will additional headcount be needed? | Plan for a shared services model. Current team supports corporate; franchisee services will need dedicated support resources as the platform rolls out. |
| 5 | **Budget parameters** — Is there a budget to work within, or should we provide budget recommendations based on what's needed? | We'll provide tiered budget recommendations (minimum viable, recommended, ideal) as part of our Phase 1 deliverables so you can make an informed decision. |

**Objective:** Walk out with clear directional answers on the 5 decisions that most impact downstream architecture, vendor selection, and project scoping.

### Block 2: Member Persona Identification (3:30 - 5:00, ~1.5 hrs)

Using the strategic direction from Block 1, identify and define the member personas that matter for Gold's Gym.

**Format:** Facilitator brings reference personas from `user-story-mapping.md` as prompts, but the room defines what's real for Gold's.

#### Activities

1. **Brainstorm** all member types that will interact with the Gold's brand (15 min)
2. **Cluster and name** primary personas — expect 4-8 (20 min)
3. **Define each persona** — goals, frustrations, primary channels, lifecycle stage (30 min)
4. **Flag personas** that need full journey mapping on Day 2 (15 min)
5. **Wrap-up** — confirm persona set, note any open questions (10 min)

#### Reference Personas (starting point, not prescriptive)

These come from the platform's user-story-mapping exercise. The room may combine, split, rename, or add to these:

- Prospective Member (researching, comparing, joining)
- Active Member (daily gym use, classes, training)
- At-Risk / Lapsed Member (declining engagement, reactivation)
- Family Account Holder (dependents, Kids Club, family billing)
- Guest / Day Pass User (trial visits, referred by member)

#### Day 1 Deliverable

Validated strategic direction + named member persona set ready for journey mapping on Day 2.

## Day 2 — Full Day (April 1, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM)

### Block 3: Member Journey Mapping (9:00 - 12:30, ~3.5 hrs)

The bulk of Day 2. Map high-level journeys for each persona identified on Day 1. This block is expected to produce **6+ journeys** — the specific journeys emerge from Day 1 strategy and personas, but examples of what may surface:

| Example Journey | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| Discover & Join | Prospect hears about Gold's → researches → tours → signs up |
| Daily Gym Visit | Member arrives → checks in → works out → leaves |
| Buy Something | eComm purchases, in-gym retail, PT packages, add-on services |
| Use Premium Amenities | Recovery room, group fitness classes, Kids Club, pool |
| Get Help / Resolve an Issue | Member has a problem → contacts support → resolution |
| I'm Losing Interest | Declining visits → outreach triggers → AI/human intervention → decision |
| Manage My Account | Billing, freeze, cancel, upgrade, payment updates |
| Family Experience | Add dependents, Kids Club booking, family billing, check-in |

#### For Each Journey, Capture:

- **Key touchpoints** — what happens at each step?
- **Channels** — app, web, in-person, AI agent, phone, SMS?
- **Pain points** — where does friction exist today?
- **Opportunities** — where can AI/automation add value?
- **Open questions** — what do we need to validate with real users?

### Key Facilitation Notes

- **Block 1:** This is a pitch, not a Q&A. Present with conviction, but pause after each major section to read the room. The 5 decision points are designed for yes/no responses — don't let them become open-ended strategy discussions. If a CEO wants to go deeper on a topic, note it and offer to revisit after the pitch is complete.
- **Block 2:** Bring the reference personas as prompts but let the room define what's real for Gold's. They may combine, split, or rename personas. The goal is _their_ persona set, not ours.
- **Block 3:** This is the largest block for a reason — don't rush it. If a journey opens up unexpected territory, explore it. Better to deeply map 6 journeys than superficially cover 10.
- **Block 4:** Keep franchisee journeys high-level. These are executive-level paths, not system-level user stories.
- **Block 5:** Vendor discussion should reference specific journey gaps identified in Blocks 3-4. Avoid abstract vendor evaluation.
- **Block 6:** Resist the urge to prioritize or scope. The goal is clean capture and clear next steps, not decisions about what to build first.
