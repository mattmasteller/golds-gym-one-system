# Gold's Gym Strategic Technology Roadmap
## Phase 1-7 Planning Framework

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** December 2025
**Prepared by:** StackHack Labs LLC

---

## Strategic Context

Gold's Gym is pivoting from a corporate-gym-focused model to a **franchise-growth-driven strategy**. The 3-5 year goal is to:

- Maintain ~50 corporate locations (steady state)
- Grow franchise network by 16-25 new gyms annually
- Develop a "best-in-class franchise playbook" to attract tier-one investors
- Return to strength & performance brand positioning

---

## Technology Decision Framework

Every technology decision should pass these filters:

| Filter | Question |
|--------|----------|
| **Strength-First** | Does it enhance the training or recovery experience? |
| **Franchise-Scalable** | Can it deploy to 150+ locations without manual configuration per club? |
| **Asset-Light** | Does it reduce headcount or automate manual processes? |
| **Data-Unified** | Does it feed one data model or create another silo? |
| **Premium-Justified** | Does it reinforce why members pay 2x HVLP pricing? |
| **Member-Facing** | Does it improve the member experience or just internal operations? |

## Strategic Imperatives by Timeline

## Engagement Phase Overview

The full transformation is structured across 7 phases:

### Phase 0 — Audit & Discovery (4–6 weeks) + 2 Week Contingency
*Current Phase*

**Work:** Knowledge transfer, stakeholder interviews, surveys, current-state stack & data review, pain-point mapping, risk & dependency register.

**Deliverables:** Current-State Map, Gap/Risk Report, Leadership Decision List.

---

### Phase 1 — Decisions & Strategy (4–6 weeks)

**Work:** Architectural guardrails, "system of one" principles, data-first & AI-readiness strategy, governance, franchise policies (required vs. optional), KPI tree.

**Deliverables:** Gold's Gym Strategy & Playbook (signed off).

---

### Phase 2 — Architecture & Blueprint (4–6 weeks)

**Work:** Target architecture, data model, integration topology, identity/permissions, reporting layer, AI enablement points, implementation wave plan.

**Deliverables:** Architecture Blueprint & Implementation Plan.

---

### Phase 3 — Resourcing, RFPs & Vendor Selection (6-8 weeks)

**Work:** RFPs, scorecards, bake-offs/POCs, commercials, SLAs, timeline & responsibility matrix (RACI), budget envelope.

**Deliverables:** Vendor Selections + Resource Plan + Master Timeline.

---

### Phase 4 — Implementation Oversight (initial 16–24 weeks)

**Work:** PMO/advisory, backlog hygiene, cutover/runbooks, data migration oversight, security & compliance checks, reporting pack stand-up.

**Deliverables:** Systems live for corporate clubs; onboarding-ready.

---

### Phase 5 — Beta with Corporate Clubs (6 - 12 weeks)

**Work:** Pilot configuration, SOP draft/iterate, training, NPS/operational metrics, fix/finish list, acceptance criteria validation.

**Deliverables:** Pilot Report, Finalized SOPs, Go/No-Go.

---

### Phase 6 — Franchisee Rollout & Onboarding (12–24 weeks)

**Work:** Rollout playbook, enablement/training content, tiered support, data checks, required vs. opt-in system packaging/pricing guidance, governance cadence.

**Deliverables:** Rollout Factory + Training Assets + Adoption Dashboards.

---

### Phase 7 — Ongoing Advisory & Governance (Monthly)

**Work:** Steering committee, roadmap refresh, vendor performance management, data/AI use-case incubation, franchise feedback loop.

**Deliverables:** Monthly scorecards & roadmap updates.

## Non-Negotiable Technology Capabilities

From stakeholder input, the new system must have:

1. **Brand-level controls** - Push configurations once, apply to all
2. **Franchise segmentation** - Each owner manages only their locations
3. **Flexible banking** - Multiple bank accounts and processors supported
4. **API-first architecture** - Integrate with gates, InBody, recovery equipment
5. **Real-time revenue visibility** - Royalty calculations based on facts
6. **Member reciprocity** - Check-ins across domestic network with tracking
7. **Tiered access control** - Recovery suite gated by membership level

---

## Unified Franchise Platform Vision

One operating system powers the entire network:

- **One membership system** across all domestic locations
- **One mobile app** for all members
- **One data model** feeding analytics and reporting
- **Brand-level controls** with franchisee-level flexibility
- **Real-time royalty visibility** based on actual transactions, not self-reporting

Corporate operates as a "super franchisee" on the same platform—no special treatment, no parallel systems.

## Risk Factors

1. **Franchisee Resistance:** Long-time operators are skeptical of corporate mandates
2. **Timeline Pressure:** Kevin says 2 more years of inaction will lose credibility permanently
3. **ABC Relationship:** 80%+ of franchisees are on ABC - any migration is complex
4. **International Complexity:** RSG Germany still owns brand; coordination required
5. **Resource Constraints:** Small corporate team, can't build large support infrastructure

---

## Revenue Opportunity

Gold's currently collects **$0 in technology fees or rebates** from franchisees. Competitors like Crunch charge:

- $5 per joint online
- Percentage of billing as rebate
- Tech fees built into franchise agreements

This could fund the support infrastructure needed without building massive teams.
