STRATEGIC TAKEAWAYS
  1. 1. Internal data and stakeholder feedback reflects misalignment and fragmentation — no unified technology direction across corporate and franchise operations
  2. 2. The market has no perfect system — every major fitness franchisor is piecing together solutions from best-in-class components
  3. 3. The market is trending data- and AI-first, pushing the industry toward Assemble over Build or Buy
  4. 4. Our recommendation: pursue an Assembly strategy — integrating best-in-class vendors around an owned data and integration layer
  5. 5. Several strategic questions remain open that must be resolved before moving to the blueprint phase
  6. 6. Website and app standardization is a prerequisite — ~90% of franchisees are on non-standard platforms, which blocks the data-first model
  7. 7. A clear business/financial model (tech fees, franchise funding) is required to sustain the Assembly approach

KEY INSIGHT

Every major fitness franchisor is assembling their technology stack from best-in-class components — not building monolithic platforms or buying single-vendor suites. Crunch, Planet Fitness, Anytime, and Xponential all follow a core-plus-integrations model with a central gym management system augmented by specialized tools for CRM, app, scheduling, and analytics.

03 BUILD v ASSEMBLE v BUY

Three paths exist for Gold's Gym to solve its technology challenges. Each carries distinct tradeoffs in cost, control, speed, and long-term ownership.

BUILD
  • Custom platform — full control, highest cost
  • Requires large dedicated engineering team
Not aligned with Gold's priorities
ASSEMBLE
  • Best-in-class vendors integrated via owned API layer
  • Moderate cost, asset-light team model
Matches Gold's DNA
BUY
  • Single vendor suite — fast but limiting
  • No vendor covers all Gold's requirements today
No single vendor fits

Gold's must own its data layer while leveraging best-in-class vendors — exactly how every successful fitness franchisor operates.

04 OUR RECOMMENDATION
THE ASSEMBLE MODEL

Gold's Gym should assemble a best-in-class technology platform: core gym management via competitive RFP, retain Motion Vibe, add specialized CRM/CDP/analytics, and build an integration layer Gold's owns. This matches Gold's asset-light DNA and keeps Gold's in control of its data and member relationships.

Next steps: Phases 1 & 2 ($52.5K combined) deliver a signed-off strategy playbook and architecture blueprint in 8-12 weeks.
