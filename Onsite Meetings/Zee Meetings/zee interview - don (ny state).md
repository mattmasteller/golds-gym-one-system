## Key Insights & Perspectives

### On the Future of Gold's Gym

**Vision for the Brand:**
> "It's more than what Gold's Gym thinks it is. It's not just strength and performance, bodybuilding. If they think that that's going to propel this brand to become a leader, you're tapping into a fraction of the market."

**The Market Opportunity:**
- People want to get healthy, not just fit
- Need coaching, guidance, and hand-holding
- Wellness, functional nutrition, and internal health measurement are the future
- Gold's should be the "quarterback" of member health

**Competitive Positioning:**
- Advocates for "premium" tier positioning
- References Kimberly Curtis in Tampa charging $100+/month
- Believes in profound value proposition over competing on price

### On Technology & Innovation

**Building Custom Solutions:**
- Developing own AI concierge for 24/7 member engagement
- Building custom CRM because industry players are inadequate
- Creating "Member Success Academy" LMS for new member onboarding
- Goal: Every new member completes ~1 hour of self-paced learning with incentives

**Member Success Academy Concept:**
- ~15 education modules on strength, fitness, wellness
- Short videos, content, and 5-question quizzes
- Call-to-action buttons after each module
- Graduates receive branded Gym bag with local business gift certificates (~$300-400 value)
- Public recognition on Gym TVs

**On Hapana:**
- Built custom Hapana software
- Paused implementation due to missing deliverables AND waiting for One System decision

### On Gold's Gym Leadership

**Critical Assessment:**
> "I'm disappointed in what has been lack of leadership and vision and strategy from the previous leaderships. You got people coming in that they're not from the fitness space and they're asked to run [it]."

**Concerns:**
> "Gold's Gym will fail in the long run. They're just, this brand will go away."

**On RSG:**
> "RSG group has the right mindset to become a franchisor in the true sense of the word. But in terms of what initiatives and vision and strategy they bring and next level opportunities for franchisees, I don't know."

> "These guys are on PTO half the time I try to reach out to them. And when they do want to do a meeting, it's like, well, let's meet in three weeks."

### On One System Project

**Strong Support:**
> "If I was the franchisor, the first thing we're doing is getting everybody on the same platform."

**Mandate Recommended:**
> "If you've got these rogue operators out there... But you know what? No, I still don't want to move. I still want to stay with who I'm with. I just say, good luck. But unfortunately, you can't stay in the brand."

**Business Case:**
- Unification required to attract PE investment
- Can't scale without systemized approach
- Current "Wild West" prevents capital attraction

### On Website Issues

**Assessment:**
> "Websites are a mess, dude. They completely butchered it. And they basically apologized to us at the leadership conference."

> "This is a train wreck. And I know they're trying to make the right moves, but I'm like, dude, you're talking about a website. Like with all the tech developers and the money that Gold's Gym has, how are you still fucking this up?"

**Priority:**
> "Nobody's going to even look at anything about a member management site until this website [is fixed]."
