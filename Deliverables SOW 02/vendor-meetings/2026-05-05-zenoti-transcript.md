## Executive takeaway (North Star lens)

Zenoti's core thesis is: **centralize the franchise network on one
unified platform** (operations + CRM/marketing + comms + payments +
data) because **AI + consistency requires data in one place**---and
because fragmented franchise tech stacks "dilute" brand and member
experience over time. They position themselves as an "enterprise-scale,
all‑in‑one" platform with robust APIs/webhooks + a reporting warehouse
(Redshift) to reduce lock‑in concerns---while still arguing the "one
platform" approach is strategically necessary to unlock AI, automation,
and standardization.

**Implication for One System:** This aligns directly to "one member
experience / one app / one data plane," *but* it raises explicit
diligence needs around **data portability, integration depth, franchise
change management, and roadmap specificity**.

## Agenda Alignment

**Near‑Term Priorities**

### 1) How Zenoti can support Gold's Gym during its transformation process

**What they answered (summary):**

- Zenoti emphasized that **consolidation is a CEO-level decision** that
  may cause short‑term franchisee attrition, but is "better long‑term,"
  and that a compelling platform is required to earn franchisee buy‑in.

- They advised leading with **the business problems** (conversion,
  collections, inconsistent messaging/brand experience), then presenting
  Zenoti as the enabling platform (not leading with features).

- They stated they can collaborate on **franchisee messaging/playbook**
  and have experience migrating multi‑platform networks into a single
  system.

**Status:** **Mostly answered.**\
**Needs more info (for North Star execution):**

- A concrete **transformation plan**: phased rollout pattern (pilot →
  regions → full network), governance model, and "minimum viable
  standardization" vs optional modules by franchisees.

### 2) Newest features/functions that have gone live since Zenoti entered fitness

**What they answered (summary):**

- They highlighted an "AI Workforce" direction (lead handling, concierge
  engagement, retention prediction, dispute/chargeback automation,
  scheduling optimization) and stressed their pace of delivery due to a
  large engineering org.

- They referenced enhanced **check‑in options** (including facial
  recognition check-in in beta) and broader platform modules
  (payroll/HRIS, internal chat, phone/IVR, marketing automation).

**Status:** **Answered at a high level.**\
**Needs more info:**

- A **fitness‑specific feature list** (what is GA vs beta vs roadmap)
  and how each maps to Gold's priorities (franchise controls,
  multi‑brand member experience, migration from current vendors).

### 3) Immediate roadmap items (requested or prioritized for current fitness customers)

**What they answered (summary):**

- They said they can share **some roadmap in writing** (with exceptions
  for proprietary areas).

- They repeatedly emphasized ability to **build missing enterprise
  features quickly** (weeks to a couple months), citing custom requests
  as typical in large migrations.

**Status:** **Partially answered.**\
**Needs more info (must-have for your One System governance):**

- A written roadmap with **timebound commitments** for fitness (next
  6/12/18 months), especially for: franchise enablement, identity/roles,
  data/warehouse, payments/collections enhancements, and member
  experience layer.

### 4) New or additional franchisor revenue streams on Zenoti

**What they answered (summary):**

- They described franchisor revenue levers: **payments margin /
  processing economics**, **technology fees** (and suggested typical
  margin ranges), and potential margining of AI/automation add-ons; they
  framed this as "profit with little incremental cost" to corporate.

**Status:** **Answered directionally.**\
**Needs more info (to operationalize & avoid franchise pushback):**

- Packaging: **what's included vs add‑on**, how fee structures work
  across franchise variance, and how they recommend positioning tech
  fees to avoid "tax" perception.

### 5) International strategy and expansion support

**What they answered (summary):**

- They stated they support **many countries**, with **regional hosting**
  for certain jurisdictions and multiple language packs; they also
  referenced payments complexity in certain markets and said they've
  built international capabilities.

**Status:** **Mostly answered.**\
**Needs more info:**

- A market-by-market matrix for Gold's near‑term targets: **data
  residency**, payment rails, language readiness, local support
  coverage, and any known fitness‑specific limitations.

### 6) Offloading/building Gold's technology support needs

**What they answered (summary):**

- They implied a partner posture: support for migrations, faster feature
  delivery, and reducing corporate burden via an all‑in‑one platform;
  they also described automation that reduces operational workload (AI
  handling calls/leads, dispute manager, etc.). mentioned teir one
  support for users, employing Zenoti SMEs with escalation when required
  and quarterly business reviews.

**Status:** **Partially answered.**\
**Needs more info (procurement-ready):**

- Clear **support model options** (tiered support, franchise helpdesk
  coverage, SLAs, escalation, named roles, implementation capacity) and
  what is included vs paid services.

**Mid‑ and Long‑Term Strategic Priorities**

### 7) Partnerships with leading franchise fitness brands (domestic & international)

**What they answered (summary):**

- They mentioned examples of enterprise fitness
  customers/implementations (e.g., **Equinox**), and referenced other
  large operators and cross‑vertical enterprise customers as proof of
  scale.

**Status:** **Partially answered.**\
**Needs more info:**

- Reference list tailored to **franchise fitness**: outcomes, deployment
  pattern, what worked/failed, and what "one system" looked like in
  practice (especially franchise enforcement and data sharing).

### 8) Integrated product strategy enabling alignment across the business

**What they answered (summary):**

- Their platform message: **marketing + CRM + comms + phone +
  operations + payroll + reporting** in one system to avoid bolt‑ons and
  enable AI.

- They described enterprise constructs: **hierarchies (corporate →
  regions → locations)**, role-based permissions, brand template
  enforcement, and cross-location settlement logic (e.g., loyalty/gift
  card style balancing).

- They also described extensibility: **APIs + webhooks + a reporting
  warehouse** and high-volume API operation as evidence of scale.

**Status:** **Answered well.**\
**Needs more info (for architecture diligence):**

- The actual **API catalog coverage** for Gold's core scenarios (join
  flow, membership lifecycle, billing/collections, check-in, offers,
  franchise reporting, app embedding), plus event taxonomy and webhook
  guarantees.

### 9) AI strategy and how it drives operational/member value

**What they answered (summary):**

- They distinguished **predictive ML** (built in-house) vs **generative
  AI** (using multiple external LLM providers depending on use case),
  and argued "AI works" because they capture communications +
  operational data in-platform.

- They discussed "AI workforce" agents for acquisition, retention,
  staffing, inventory, disputes, and executive insights; and noted AI
  cost optimization is still a workstream (caching/data retrieval
  efficiency).

- They stated predictive models use **your data to help you** (not to
  help another operator), while some generative patterns may benefit
  from cross-business learning.

**Status:** **Answered strongly conceptually.**\
**Needs more info (security/legal/brand risk controls):**

- Formal **data governance**: what trains what, tenant isolation
  guarantees, retention, opt‑in/out for cross-tenant generative
  improvement, and how franchise data is partitioned across the
  hierarchy.

### 10) Payments platform: efficiency, growth, member experience

**What they answered (summary):**

- They positioned payments as a franchisor lever: potential cost
  advantage vs legacy processors and the ability to build margin-sharing
  models.

- They described collections improvements as part of the platform
  narrative (e.g., better handling of bank account validity and reducing
  failures as an operational issue).

**Status:** **Partially answered.**\
**Needs more info:**

- Payments diligence checklist: supported payment types, reconciliation
  flows, chargeback SLAs, tokenization, fallback processors, settlement
  timing, and franchise accounting integration---plus how this
  integrates with Gold's desired "one member account" across locations.

### 11) Data platform: efficiency, growth, and revenue opportunities

**What they answered (summary):**

- They described a reporting/data approach using **Amazon Redshift** as
  a normalized dataset for customer analytics and external BI, with
  export options and near-real-time latency considerations at scale.

- They stated customers can also pull data into their own lake and
  combine it with other datasets, and they emphasized APIs/webhooks for
  event-driven data movement.

**Status:** **Answered well conceptually.**\
**Needs more info:**

- Data contract specifics: schema ownership/versioning, PII handling,
  latency SLAs, event completeness, and how "franchisee vs corporate"
  access is enforced for North Star governance.
