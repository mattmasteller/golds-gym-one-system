01 ASSEMBLE = OWN DATA LAYER + CONSUMER EXPERIENCE

Three fundamental approaches exist. Gold's needs the one that matches its DNA: asset-light, franchise-ready, and data-first.

BUY
  • What: Select a single vendor to provide all functionality
  • Control: Limited — locked into vendor's roadmap
  • Risk: No single vendor covers Gold's requirements today
Limited control, limited flexibility
ASSEMBLE
  • What: Own data layer and consumer experience, integrate best-in-class vendors per category
  • Control: Full — own the data model, integration layer, and member-facing experiences
  • Model: Modular and flexible — swap vendors without rebuilding the platform
Asset-light. Matches Gold's DNA.
BUILD
  • What: Custom-build a proprietary platform from scratch
  • Control: Complete — but at extreme cost and timeline
  • Risk: Requires large engineering org Gold's doesn't have
Expensive and slow
THE CORE PRINCIPLE

We assemble the platform — we do not rent it. Gold's owns the data, owns the consumer experience, and owns the integration layer that connects everything. Vendors are best-in-class components that plug into Gold's platform. If a vendor underperforms, it gets replaced without rebuilding the system.

WHAT GOLD'S OWNS
  • Data layer — single source of truth across all locations
  • Consumer experience — website, app, and all member touchpoints
  • Integration layer — the API backbone that connects all vendors
  • Brand standards — consistent experience across the franchise network
WHAT VENDORS PROVIDE
  • Best-in-class functionality per category (CRM, billing, scheduling, etc.)
  • Their own infrastructure, support, and product development
  • APIs and integrations that conform to Gold's standards
  • Innovation in their domain — Gold's gets the benefit without the build cost

02 CONSUMER TOUCHPOINTS ACROSS THE JOURNEY

Technology touches every stage of the consumer journey. The platform must coordinate all of these touchpoints into a single, seamless experience.

PRE-MEMBER

Awareness & Engagement

  • Digital ads & social media
  • Website & landing pages
  • Content & programs (outside the gym)
  • Guest pass & trial experiences
  • CRM & lead nurturing
JOINING

Conversion & Onboarding

  • Join online flow
  • Pricing & membership options
  • Onboarding experience
  • First-visit scheduling
  • App download & setup
MEMBER

Retention & Engagement

  • Mobile app experience
  • In-club technology (gate, POS, scheduling)
  • Training programs & tracking
  • Community & social features
  • Travel pass & multi-location access

Key insight: The experience is only as strong as its weakest touchpoint. A great app means nothing if the join-online flow is broken. A great website means nothing if the in-club experience doesn't match. Every stage requires coordinated technology — and that coordination is exactly what the Assemble strategy delivers.

03 OWN THE WEBSITE & CONSUMER APP EXPERIENCE

The website and the app are the front door to the Gold's Gym brand. They must be owned — not rented from a vendor, not fragmented across franchisees.

WHY OWNERSHIP MATTERS
  • Brand control — the front door sets the tone for everything that follows
  • Data ownership — every interaction generates data Gold's needs to own
  • Roadmap control — Gold's decides what gets built, not a vendor's PM team
  • Consumer relationship — the brand-to-consumer connection cannot be mediated by a third party
WHAT OWNERSHIP REQUIRES
  • Development — internal or dedicated partner for website and app
  • Operations — ongoing management, content, and optimization
  • Roadmap alignment — marketing, product, and technology working together
  • Franchise integration — every franchisee on the same platform, with local flexibility
"If we don't own the front door, we don't own the customer." Strategic Principle

The website and app are positioned as the tip of the spear — the most visible expression of the platform strategy and the first place consumers interact with the brand.
