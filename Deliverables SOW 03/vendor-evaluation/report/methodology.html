01 THREE-LAYER SCORING PROCESS
LAYER A — DETERMINISTIC BASE (RULES)

Every vendor answered the same locked 14-tab workbook, choosing from a controlled vocabulary (Support Level × Availability). Each answer maps to a base score through the published lookup table below — fully reproducible from the workbook alone, with zero human or model judgment.

LAYER B — LLM EVIDENCE EXTRACTION

A pinned model (claude-sonnet-5, temperature 0, versioned prompts) reads the vendor's written narrative and demo transcripts and may propose an adjustment of at most ±1 to the base score — only with verbatim, page-cited quotes. Proposals without citable evidence are automatically discarded. Every model call is archived for audit.

LAYER C — HUMAN RATIFICATION

Subject-matter experts review each item in an adjudication console showing the base score, the model's proposal, and all quoted evidence — then ratify or override with a note. No score is final until a human ratifies it. Every final score carries full provenance: contributing workbook rows, evidence quotes, reviewer, and timestamp.

THRESHOLD GATES FIRST

Before any weighting, each vendor is assessed against seven pass/fail threshold requirements (data ownership, real-time APIs, franchise architecture, export commitment, U.S. support, POS, open ecosystem). A fail or unclear verdict escalates to the Decision Council regardless of the weighted score.

04 AUDITABILITY
EVERY SCORE IS TRACEABLE

Each item score links to the workbook rows, document pages, or transcript timestamps that produced it (see Evidence Appendix). The scoring database, all model calls, and the lookup tables are preserved so the entire evaluation re-runs deterministically end-to-end.

INDEPENDENT MATH CHECK

The companion Excel export recomputes category and total scores with live spreadsheet formulas from the item level — Gold's can audit the arithmetic without any of our tooling.
