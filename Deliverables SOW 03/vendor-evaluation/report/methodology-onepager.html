The question we answer

Five vendors responded to the Membership Management System RFI. Which one is the best fit — for the Gold's brand overall and for each business group — and can we prove it rather than assert it? Every number in the final report traces to a locked workbook answer, a quoted page of a vendor's written response, or a time-stamped moment in a demo recording.

Three layers, one auditable score

A
Deterministic rules
Every vendor filled in the same locked 14-tab workbook using a controlled vocabulary. Each answer maps to a 0–5 base score through a published lookup table — no judgment involved, 100% reproducible.
→
B
AI evidence extraction
A pinned model (temperature 0, versioned prompts) reads each vendor's written response and demo transcripts. It may propose at most a ±1 adjustment — only with verbatim, page-cited quotes. No citation, no adjustment. Every model call is archived.
→
C
Human ratification
Gold's SMEs review each item — base score, AI proposal, and quoted evidence side by side — and ratify or override with a note. No score is final until a person signs it.

GATES FIRST 7 pass/fail thresholds before any weighting

Full data ownership (contractual) · Real-time API access (REST + webhooks) · Multi-location franchise architecture (native) · Documented data export & migration commitment · U.S. market support · POS / retail integrated or tightly coupled · Open ecosystem / headless compatibility.

Any fail or unclear verdict escalates to the Decision Council regardless of the weighted score.

Why the result is defensible

Deterministic & re-runnableThe entire pipeline re-runs end-to-end to the same numbers. The lookup tables, weights, prompts, and model version are all published in the methodology.
Sensitivity-testedWe re-run the ranking under ±5pp weight shifts per category, three high-signal multipliers, and each scoring layer alone — and report exactly where (if anywhere) the order changes.
Independently checkableAn Excel export recomputes every category and total with live spreadsheet formulas, and the evidence appendix links every score to its quotes. Gold's can audit without our tooling.
Business-group lens. Alongside the brand-level ranking, department demo scores (weighted: Must-have ×3, Nice-to-have ×1) produce a vendor × business-group heat map — so Membership Ops, Billing, Marketing, ProShop, and Technology each see their own best fit, not just the overall winner.
