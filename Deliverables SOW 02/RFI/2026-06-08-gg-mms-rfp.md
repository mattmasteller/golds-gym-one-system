## 0. Instructions to Respondents

This Request for Proposal invites **finalist vendors** — selected from responses to the companion RFI (GG-2026-MMS-RFI-001) and the departmental discovery/demonstration sessions — to submit a **binding proposal** for a Member Management System (MMS) and tightly-coupled retail Point of Sale (POS) for Gold's Gym.

- **Builds on the RFI; does not repeat it.** Full strategic context, functional scope, technical/API requirements, and integration detail live in the RFI. This RFP references them rather than restating them. Where your RFP response differs from or supersedes your RFI response, say so explicitly.
- **Binding offer.** Responses to this RFP are binding for the validity period stated in §9.1 and are intended to form the basis of a contract. Representations made in your RFI response (including the product-continuity and sunset disclosures of RFI §12.6) are incorporated by reference and carried forward as binding.
- **NDA in force.** The mutual NDA executed at the RFI stage remains in effect. No new NDA is required unless Gold's provides one.
- **Single point of contact.** All communication runs through the contact named above. Direct contact with any other Gold's stakeholder outside this channel may disqualify the respondent.
- **Q&A protocol.** Submit questions in writing by the deadline in §9. Material questions and answers are distributed to all finalist vendors.
- **Response format.** Two parts, submitted as separate, clearly labeled documents: (1) the **Commercial Proposal** (§4 — pricing, TCO, and the completed pricing workbook, Appendix A); and (2) the **Compliance & Terms Response** (mandatory-requirements confirmation Appendix B; contract-terms acceptance matrix Appendix C; SLA and implementation commitments). Confirm or qualify every mandatory requirement and contract term.
- **No hidden fees.** Any fee, charge, or commercial condition not disclosed in your Commercial Proposal is presumed waived. Completeness is itself an evaluation factor.

## 4. Commercial Proposal & Pricing

This is the core of the RFP and the content deliberately **not** requested at the RFI stage. Submit as a separate Commercial Proposal document, with the pricing workbook (Appendix A) completed in full. Present your own pricing structure **in addition to** the workbook so Gold's can compare across vendors without losing your native model.

- **4.1 Pricing model.** Disclose every component — per-location, per-member, per-transaction, implementation, integration, professional services, and support-tier fees. State which components are negotiable. No hidden line items (§0).
- **4.2 Five-year TCO.** Line-item total cost of ownership over five years at projected network scale, with stated member-growth and transaction-volume assumptions. Use the assumptions Gold's provides for an apples-to-apples baseline, then add your own scenario if you wish.
- **4.3 A-to-Z menu.** A complete menu of all available modules and services — including items Gold's has not requested — so Gold's can see optional/negotiable additions. Mark each as in-scope, optional, or future.
- **4.4 Implementation & migration fees.** One-time fees broken out from recurring; migration-assistance fees disclosed even if discounted to zero.
- **4.5 Volume incentives.** Scale discounts as Gold's onboards the full network; propose the discount curve by location count.
- **4.6 Member technology fee / revenue share.** Gold's intends to introduce a member technology fee. State your willingness to support, white-label, or revenue-share, and the mechanics.
- **4.7 Price lock & escalators.** Caps on annual increases, benchmark used (CPI or fixed %), and renewal-price mechanics.
- **4.8 API & integration economics.** Whether APIs/webhooks are included in base or metered; per-call fees at scale, rate-limit tiers, integration-marketplace fees, and any premium event-stream fees.

> 🔧 **[BUSINESS GROUP TO DEFINE — Finance & Billing]:** TCO baseline assumptions (member counts, growth, transaction volume), budget guardrails, and the pricing-workbook template (Appendix A).

## 5. Service Levels (Binding SLA Commitments)

State numerical, contractually binding commitments. Check-in and billing cannot be down during peak gym hours.

- **5.1 Uptime.** Gold's target is **99.9%**. State your committed uptime and the measurement method.
- **5.2 Response & resolution by severity.** Critical (system down; billing or check-in unavailable): response < 1 hour. High: < 4 hours. Medium: < 1 business day. Low: next planned release. Confirm or counter each.
- **5.3 Maintenance windows.** Off-peak only, advance notice, none during defined peak gym hours.
- **5.4 Disaster recovery.** Committed RTO and RPO (numeric), backup frequency, geographic redundancy, and last tested-recovery date.
- **5.5 Service credits.** Credit structure for missed SLAs, escalating with severity and duration.

> 🔧 **[BUSINESS GROUP TO DEFINE — IT/Security + Ryan]:** Final peak-hours definition and any threshold adjustments.

## 6. Implementation, Migration & Change Management (Committed Plan)

Provide a committed, resourced plan — not an approach narrative (that was the RFI).

- **6.1 Phased rollout.** Pilot → regional wave → full network, incumbent running in parallel until each wave is verified stable. Provide the wave structure, dates, and exit criteria per wave.
- **6.2 Vendor-led data migration.** Committed migration of member master, transaction history, payment tokens, freezes/pauses, custom attributes, and historical reporting data, with validation and rollback plans. Gold's validates; vendor executes.
- **6.3 Training & change management.** Deliverables, train-the-trainer model, and role-specific tracks (front desk, PT staff, manager, franchisee owner, corporate analyst).
- **6.4 Franchisee adoption.** Committed support for Gold's-led change management across independent operators; reference comparable multi-location rollouts.
- **6.5 Project staffing & RACI.** Named team members with credentials and tenure, PM continuity through go-live, escalation path, and a RACI across vendor, Gold's, and the StackHack project team. Continuity of named staff is a commitment, not a hope.

## 7. Contract Terms (Binding — To Be Negotiated With Counsel)

These were "reference only" in the RFI. Here they are the **terms Gold's intends to contract on.** Confirm acceptance, or qualify with specific proposed language, in Appendix C. Final contract drafted with Gold's counsel.

- **7.1 Data ownership.** Gold's owns all member data, schema, derived data, and outputs. No vendor use of Gold's data for product development, model training, or aggregated benchmarking without explicit written consent.
- **7.2 Termination & data return.** On termination for any reason, the vendor returns all data in agreed formats within a defined window and cooperates with the successor system at agreed rates.
- **7.3 IP & confidentiality.** Mutual confidentiality (the executed NDA survives). Gold's brand, franchise-network composition, and member-level data are Gold's confidential information regardless of how accessed.
- **7.4 Indemnification & liability.** Standard commercial terms; security-breach indemnification carved out from any general liability cap.
- **7.5 Renewal & pricing.** Renewal mechanics, escalator caps (per §4.7), and Gold's right to re-bid at end of term without penalty.
- **7.6 API & integration access protections.** API pricing, rate limits, webhook coverage, and integration-marketplace policies cannot be unilaterally degraded during the term; material changes require advance notice and mutual agreement.
- **7.7 Product continuity & no forced migration.** The vendor will not sunset the contracted platform, or migrate/consolidate Gold's onto a different platform, without (a) Gold's prior written consent; (b) advance notice of no less than [12 months — counsel to set]; (c) feature and data parity on any successor platform, with migration performed and **funded by the vendor**; and (d) Gold's right, if it does not consent to the destination platform, to terminate without penalty and receive full data return and migration cooperation per §7.2. The sunset/continuity representations from RFI §12.6 are incorporated by reference; a material omission is a breach. Price, SLA, and continuity protections survive on any successor platform.

## 10. Evaluation & Award

Gold's will evaluate binding proposals against the factors below. The weighting deliberately favors integration, data control, and continuity over feature breadth and headline price. **Detailed scoring weights are maintained internally** (`2026-05-20-gg-mms-evaluation-rubric-internal.md`) and are not published in this RFP.

Evaluation factors:

- **Commercial value** — total five-year TCO, pricing transparency, completeness (no hidden fees), volume incentives, tech-fee/revenue-share, and API economics.
- **Mandatory-requirement compliance** — full vs. partial, and the credibility of any remediation commitments.
- **Data ownership & continuity** — contractual data ownership, portability, and the strength of the §7.7 no-forced-migration protections.
- **Reliability & SLA** — committed uptime, severity response/resolution, DR, and credits.
- **Implementation confidence** — resourced plan, named-team continuity, migration rigor, and comparable-scale references.
- **Contract terms** — acceptance of Gold's terms (§7) vs. extent of qualification required.
- **Demonstrated capability** — evidence from proof-of-capability demonstrations and reference checks.

Process: proposal review → reference checks → demonstrations (if outstanding) → selection → contract negotiation. Gold's reserves the right to negotiate with one or more finalists and to decline all proposals.
