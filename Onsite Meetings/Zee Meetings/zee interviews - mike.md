# Zee Interviews Summary - Mike Hansen

## Overview

This document summarizes three franchisee interviews conducted by Mike Hansen as part of the Gold's Gym One System discovery process. These interviews gathered insights on current technology stacks, business processes, pain points, and perspectives on the future franchise model.

---

## Interview Summaries

### 1. Don - Gold's Gym New York State (Newburgh & Middletown)

**Background:**
- Operates 2 locations: Newburgh (76,000 sq ft, ~13,000 members) and Middletown (53,000 sq ft)
- Family business started by his mother in 1986 - almost 40 years of operation
- President of the Gold's Gym Franchisee Association (GGFA)

**Tech Stack:**
- ABC Financial for billing
- Glean Tap as CRM
- Built custom app with Motion Vibe; transitioning to new Gold's app
- Own website (goldsgymsOC) - not on corporate site

**Key Themes:**
- **Wellness-focused vision**: Operates Align Life natural healthcare clinic inside the Gym (chiropractic, functional nutrition, blood work)
- **Building custom technology**: Developing own AI concierge, custom CRM, member LMS ("Member Success Academy")
- **Critical of Gold's leadership**: Believes previous leadership lacked vision; concerned brand will fail without embracing wellness
- **Future model**: Advocates for wellness + strength integration - not just bodybuilding. Believes Gold's should lean into premium tier with coaching, functional nutrition, and personalized health
- **Strong opinions on One System**: Wants franchisees forced onto unified platform - "good luck" if they don't comply; sees it as prerequisite for PE investment

---

### 2. Mike Williams - Gold's Gym Washington State (Seattle/Vancouver)

**Background:**
- 6 clubs (potentially 7), 21 years in franchise
- Prior career: 16 years at Cigna Healthcare (VP of new business, mergers & acquisitions)
- Clubs range from 24,000-43,000 sq ft; some with pools and swim teams

**Tech Stack:**
- ABC Financial (Ignite)
- Club OS for CRM/lead management
- **On corporate Gold's website** (only franchisee interviewed still using it)
- No app currently

**Key Themes:**
- **Website problems explained in detail**: Lost leads when users clicked to ABC join page; no tracking, no retargeting, no abandoned cart data. Recently moved to new online join system and already seeing doubled leads
- **No-sales model**: Transitioned away from traditional sales teams pre-COVID; uses iPad/online join model
- **Pricing**: $58-$95/month depending on tier and location
- **Critical of franchise structure**: "They haven't figured out how to be a franchisor" - systems built for company-owned gyms, not franchisees
- **Two perspectives**: Can offer both what he'd want for himself AND what brand should do

---

### 3. Josh - Gold's Gym Washington DC Area

**Background:**
- 20 years at Gold's corporate as Regional VP of US West
- Now operates 6+ franchise clubs (DC area, based in San Antonio)
- Clubs are 17,000-25,000 sq ft

**Tech Stack:**
- ABC Financial (full service)
- HubSpot for CRM
- Fitness BI for dashboards/KPIs (showed demo of "GymStats")
- Replify (AI/Hey Libby) for member communication
- ADP for HR
- Own website (not corporate)

**Key Themes:**
- **Simplest model**: "Very basic" - mostly fitness floor, no group exercise in new builds, minimal complexity
- **Strong ABC advocate**: Believes ABC is most reliable; concerned about risk of switching to newer platforms. "I don't know how another company would be considered"
- **Separates issues**: ABC for billing is reliable; Gym Sales (CRM) is the problem - don't bundle them together
- **Website frustrations**: Built own site for tracking, SEO, speed - corporate site had "lack of flexibility, lack of tracking, lack of info"
- **Tech stack simplicity**: Wants technology that's "non-friction" and member-friendly
- **Data-driven**: Uses weighted sales forecasting, rankings, budget variance dashboards via Fitness BI

## Common Themes

1. **Website is the #1 Problem**: All three emphasize the corporate website issues must be solved before other systems. Problems include lack of tracking, no retargeting, no abandoned cart capture, and inflexibility.

2. **ABC Financial Dominance**: All three use ABC for member management/billing and see it as reliable. The debate is around CRM and other ancillary systems.

3. **Franchisee Autonomy vs. Unification**: Tension between desire for brand unification and need for franchisee flexibility to operate their unique models.

4. **Vision Uncertainty**: Questions about what the "Gold's Gym of the future" looks like - strength/performance only, or expanded into wellness?

5. **Technology as Enabler**: Agreement that tech stack should support business strategy, not drive it. Need clarity on franchise model first.
