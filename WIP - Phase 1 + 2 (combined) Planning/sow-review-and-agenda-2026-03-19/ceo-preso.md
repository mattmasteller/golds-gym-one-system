### **Executive Summary: The Gold’s Gym of the Future**

Gold’s Gym is entering its next era. **Project Strong** is the unified franchise model and operating system that will define the Gold’s Gym of the Future, purpose-built to reclaim category leadership in **strength, performance, and the aspirational fitness culture.**

This next-generation Gold’s Gym is a **30,000 sq ft strength-forward flagship**, engineered for the modern athlete, bodybuilder, and performance-driven member. Rather than replicating the generic amenities of HVLP clubs or big-box multipurpose facilities, Gold’s Gym 2.0 focuses on what Gold’s has always done best: **unmatched strength training, elite performance environments, and a culture built around results which will bring in recovery and precision fitness.**

## **A Purpose-Built Strength & Performance Ecosystem**

The Strong Gold’s Gym centers on delivering the **best strength-training experience in the world**, featuring:

* **Massive free weight zones** with expanded dumbbell ranges, Olympic platforms, specialty bars, and strength machines not found in traditional clubs.
* **Performance turf and functional training areas** designed to support HYROX-style training, conditioning, and athletic development.
* **A dedicated Small Group Performance Studio**, offering classes fully aligned with strength, conditioning, and athletic performance.
* **Gold’s Gym Posing Room**, a heritage-based amenity supporting bodybuilders, physique athletes, and creators.
* **Content-enabled spaces**, with strategically designed (15% or less of total floor space) zones optimized for recording workouts, creator-friendly lighting, and social engagement.

This concept caters to the **serious fitness enthusiast** → bodybuilders, HYROX competitors, aspiring athletes, fitness influencers, and experienced gym-goers, creating a training environment unavailable at EOS, Crunch, or general HVLP formats and only lightly touched by high-end luxury clubs.

## **Engineered Recovery, Biohacking & Precision Fitness**

To complement intense training, every club integrates a **full recovery & biohacking suite**, including but not limited too:

* Cold plunge & contrast therapies
* Infrared sauna & red light therapy
* Compression systems
* Percussive therapy stations
* Additional precision-wellness modalities as they evolve

These services elevate the member journey from “workout” to **performance lifestyle**, supported by **onsite nutrition and supplements**, Gold’s retail, and integrated guidance through the Gold’s Gym App.

This also makes the fitness enthusiasts “off days” an on day at a Gold’s Gym. This offering will only be available with the premier level membership and provide full access via the app.

## **Unified Technology for a Unified Franchise System**

Strong Gold’s Gym is anchored by **one connected technology system** powering:

* **Membership access levels**
* **Recovery & class access control**
* **Franchise-wide programming and equipment standards**
* **Operational consistency across all new locations**
* **Unified data systems across the entire funnel**

The system ensures every new franchise launches with **one operating model, one technology ecosystem, and one brand-standard playbook.**

The pricing of the Strong Gold’s Gym model is not low price but more specialized with no membership under $49.95 and premier level memberships starting at $69.95. These will vary some based on markets but the idea is to be double the price of HVLP while still remaining below the $90 pricing segments.

## **Target Market & Membership Model**

The Strong Gold’s Gym is **not** designed for the general population searching for spas, pools, childcare, or low-price, high-volume amenities. Instead, it is designed for the **modern performance consumer**, the individual who wants a premium, serious training environment without the crowds and compromises of HVLP clubs.

**Membership Tiers:**

1. **Basic** – Full gym access excluding recovery suite and small-group performance classes.
2. **Performance+** – All-access membership including recovery suite, classes, and **entry-level HYROX event integration option in partnership with Hyrox.**
3. **Optional Local Add-Ons** – Outdoor training zones, specialty clinics, or advanced recovery upgrades where applicable and strategic retail partners.

## **Operational Model & Staffing Structure**

Each club will operate with a staff light model including:

* **GM + AGM leadership team**
* **Front desk + member services staff**
* **Hybrid in-house and outsourced training department** to support revenue, performance coaching, and HYROX-style programming
* **Standardized marketing playbooks and national brand partnerships** for demand generation and member acquisition and shared resource models.

The staffing and cost structure is designed to be **mid-range**, allowing the brand to deliver premium experiences without the overhead of legacy big-box models.

## **A Category-Defining Position for the Next Decade**

Gold’s Gym 2.0 positions the brand at the forefront of:

* **Strength culture**
* **Performance training**
* **Recovery & biohacking**
* **Athlete-inspired group training**
* **Fitness content creation and social influence**
* **Precision health & lifestyle optimization**

Project Strong is not just a redesign→it is a **strategic repositioning** to own the fastest-growing segment in strength and fitness: performance-driven, experience-seeking athletes and creators who want the very best environment to train, recover, and belong.

Gold’s Gym created the modern gym industry. **Gold’s Gym Strong will bring it back and redefine it for the modern era fitness consumer.**  Belonging to Golds means something!
