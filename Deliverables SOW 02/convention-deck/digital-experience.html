01 WEBSITE STRATEGY

The website must serve two masters: corporate needs centralized data and brand control, while franchisees need pricing flexibility, local offers, and the ability to run their business. The answer is centralized data with decentralized execution.

CORPORATE CONTROLS
  • Data & analytics — all website activity flows into the central data layer
  • Brand structure — consistent design, navigation, and user experience
  • SEO & performance — centrally managed for maximum visibility
  • Join online flow — standardized conversion path across all locations
FRANCHISEE CONTROLS
  • Pricing & membership options — set by each franchisee
  • Local offers & promotions — create and manage campaigns
  • Landing pages — build custom pages for local marketing
  • Content & imagery — localized within brand guidelines
CENTRALIZED DATA, DECENTRALIZED EXECUTION

Corporate sees everything. Franchisees control their local experience. Approved agencies get access to the platform, not ownership of it. This model ensures brand consistency while preserving the entrepreneurial flexibility that franchisees require.

AGENCY MODEL
  • Approved vendor list for franchisee marketing partners
  • Agencies operate within the platform — no separate websites
  • Access is granted, not ownership — Gold's controls the infrastructure
  • Experimentation and A/B testing capabilities built into the platform
02 APP STRATEGY

The app must evolve from a member utility tool into the consumer engagement platform for Gold's Gym — the daily touchpoint that keeps the brand relevant between gym visits.

CURRENT STATE

Member utility tool

  • Check-in functionality
  • Class schedules
  • Basic account management
  • 20-30+ separate apps in app stores
Engagement Level
Low
FUTURE STATE

Consumer engagement platform

  • Personalized content & programs
  • Training tracking & progress
  • Community & social features
  • Pre-member engagement (before joining)
  • In-club & out-of-club experiences
Engagement Level
High

Key shift: The app becomes the brand experience, not just a feature. It is the daily touchpoint for content, training, community, and engagement — both inside and outside the gym. One app, all domestic members, unified experience.

03 DIGITAL PRODUCTS OUTSIDE THE FOUR WALLS

The Gold's Gym brand extends beyond the physical gym. Digital products create new revenue streams, generate leads, and keep the brand relevant to consumers who may never set foot in a location.

BODYBUILDING PROGRAMS

Leverage Gold's unmatched authority in bodybuilding with structured programs from top athletes and coaches. Premium content that no other gym brand can credibly deliver.

TRAINING PLANS

Personalized training plans powered by the data layer — from beginner to advanced, strength to performance. Available to members and non-members alike.

VIDEO CONTENT

On-demand and live training content. Drive traffic via ads, capture users before they ever walk into a gym, and create a digital relationship that converts to membership.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Digital products serve three purposes: lead generation (capture consumers before they join), brand extension (reach people who may never visit a gym), and new revenue streams (subscriptions, premium content, partnerships). The brand extends beyond the physical gym — the platform must too.
