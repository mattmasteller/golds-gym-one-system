01 THE STRATEGIC NORTH STAR FROM HQ

The December 2025 onsite sessions at Gold's Gym HQ set the strategic frame for everything in this deck. Leadership was unambiguous: Gold's is shifting from a corporate-gym-focused model to a franchise-growth-driven strategy — and the technology platform must make that shift possible.

STRATEGIC DIRECTION
  • Franchise growth is the focus — target 16–25 new franchise gyms per year; corporate gyms stay steady (~50 locations)
  • Best-in-class franchise playbook — the asset that attracts tier-one franchisee candidates (investment groups, multi-unit operators)
  • Strength & performance DNA — return to the brand's core, not "everything for everyone"
  • Asset-light operations — technology enables self-service and automation, not headcount growth
THE GYM OF THE FUTURE
  • 25,000–35,000 sq ft footprint, GM + AGM lean staffing model
  • Tech-forward sales — self-service, online sign-ups, QR-code access
  • Strength / performance focus with recovery amenities — elevated pricing ($55–70+) vs. HVLP competitors
  • Templates exist today — Thornton (CO) and Burnett Road (Austin)
"Three years from now, we want to have a much better traditional franchiser playbook to deploy to franchisees... if we're gonna get real blue chip franchise candidates to come into this brand, we have to offer what would be considered table stakes." Brad (co-CEO) — Executive Session, December 2025
02 WHAT WE HEARD — EIGHT DEPARTMENTS

Every department told a version of the same story: strong brand, clear intent, and technology that can't keep up. Each session surfaced the must-haves that now drive the platform requirements and the RFP evaluation criteria.

EXECUTIVE (CO-CEOs)
  • Franchise-first strategy; asset-light support model
  • Scalable playbook to win tier-one candidates
  • Technology must enable the business, not burden it

"We want to be best-in-class to attract tier-one franchise candidates."

OPERATIONS
  • 48 corporate clubs configured by a single specialist — manual everything
  • Needs promo scheduling with future dates and auto-expiry
  • Bulk configuration changes; reliable cancellation APIs

"Friday at 4pm fire drills" — promos require manual day-of configuration.

BILLING & FINANCE
  • Franchise billing runs on manual spreadsheets
  • No revenue visibility until franchisees self-report
  • Needs automated royalty calculation, reconciliation, multi-processor banking

"We're chasing payments via spreadsheets and have no visibility until they self-report."

MARKETING
  • CAC $88–125 vs. $60–70 industry benchmark
  • No SMS capability — blocked by legal on compliance gaps
  • Needs ad-click-to-in-gym attribution and referral tracking

"We can't send SMS — legal blocked it due to compliance gaps."

DIGITAL MARKETING
  • 90%+ of franchisees run their own websites; 20–30+ Gold's apps in app stores
  • Join Online backlog: 24 franchise groups at 3 weeks of manual onboarding each
  • CDP planned — but data normalization must come first

"24 franchise groups backlogged because each requires 3-week manual onboarding."

FITNESS
  • Motion Vibe is mission-critical for group exercise — non-negotiable integration
  • PT sessions can't be booked remotely — must be in-gym on the network
  • Demotu under evaluation for movement assessments; InBody integration needed

"PTs can't book sessions unless they're physically in the gym on our network."

FRANCHISING
  • 150+ domestic franchise locations; growth hinges on a compelling playbook
  • $0 collected in tech fees today — competitors charge $5+ per transaction
  • Needs travel pass / reciprocity, performance dashboards, brand-level controls

"We've been talking about this for four years now... If we wait two years, they're going to keep doing their own thing."

IT
  • API-first architecture is the baseline requirement
  • SSO / identity management and MFA across the stack
  • Must integrate with existing infrastructure (AWS, Meraki)

"Every system is a silo — data doesn't flow."

03 FIVE THEMES, SIX QUICK WINS
CROSS-DEPARTMENT THEMES
  • "We're not best in class" — every department acknowledged the gap vs. competitor playbooks (Crunch, Anytime, Planet)
  • Normalize first — standardized naming, GL structures, and membership configurations are the prerequisite for any system working at scale
  • No call centers — asset-light means self-service and automation, not large support teams
  • Value, not mandates — franchisees must experience the platform as an upgrade; adoption is earned
  • Website & app are the trust-builders — near-term wins that demonstrate value before the full migration

NON-NEGOTIABLES FROM THE SESSIONS
  • Brand-level controls — push configurations once, apply to all
  • Franchise segmentation — each owner manages only their gyms
  • API-first architecture — gates, towel trackers, InBody, and beyond
  • Real-time revenue visibility — royalties from facts, not self-reporting
  • Flexible banking, configurable permissions, sales tax handling
  • Motion Vibe integration — group exercise scheduling is mission-critical

When a vendor scores poorly on a criterion, we know exactly which business objective is at risk — and when priorities shift, we know exactly which requirements move with them.
