01 VISION OF GOLD'S GYM

The Gold's Strong Model defines what this brand is — and what it is not. Every technology decision must pass through this framework.

WHAT GOLD'S IS
  • A strength and performance brand with 50+ years of heritage
  • A franchise-growth company targeting 16-25 new locations annually
  • A premium experience priced above HVLP competitors ($55-70+)
  • A lean, technology-enabled operation (GM + AGM staffing model)
WHAT GOLD'S IS NOT
  • A general-purpose fitness center for casual exercisers
  • A low-price, high-volume (HVLP) competitor
  • A big-box gym with pools, spas, and childcare
  • A corporate-gym-focused business
FIVE STRATEGIC PILLARS
1. Strength Dominance

Massive free weight zones, Olympic platforms, performance turf, HYROX-style training. No pools. No childcare. No HVLP dilution.

2. Recovery as Revenue

Cold plunge, infrared sauna, red light therapy, compression systems. Tier-gated to drive premium membership adoption.

3. Lean Operations

GM + AGM only. Technology must reduce headcount, not create it. Self-service check-in, booking, and payment are mandatory.

4. Unified Platform

One membership system, one app, one data model. Brand controls with franchise flexibility. Real-time royalty visibility.

5. Premium Positioning

2x HVLP pricing ($49.95-$69.95+). Attracts tier-one franchise candidates who expect turnkey operations and strong unit economics.

Facility Template

~30,000 sq ft · QR/gate access · Recovery suite · Performance studio · Content zones

02 THE ASSEMBLE MODEL

Assemble best-in-class components connected by an integration layer that Gold's owns. Each component is selected for its category strength, not forced into a monolithic suite.

Component Approach Current State Recommendation
Core Gym Management Competitive RFP ABC (franchise) / Motionsoft (corporate) Select unified platform via Phase 3 RFP
Group Exercise Retain & Integrate Motion Vibe (4.5/5 score) Keep Motion Vibe — non-negotiable per Fitness dept
CRM & Marketing Evaluate & Upgrade HubSpot (corporate only, 2.5/5) Fitness-specific CRM with SMS compliance
CDP Add New None Unified member data platform (Segment, Treasure Data, etc.)
Member App Consolidate 20-30+ fragmented apps Single branded app for all domestic members
Access Control Standardize Mixed QR/gate systems Unified access with tiered recovery suite gating
Data & Analytics Build Layer No centralized reporting Data lake + BI dashboards with real-time franchise visibility
OWN YOUR DATA

The integration layer connecting these components is the most strategic asset. Gold's must own and control how data flows between systems. This prevents vendor lock-in, enables AI-readiness, and ensures that switching any individual component doesn't require rebuilding the entire stack. This layer also serves as build-ready foundations — regardless of whether Gold's assembles or eventually builds, owning data and APIs is the prerequisite for either path.

KEY PRINCIPLES
  • API-first architecture — every system connects via standard APIs
  • Data ownership — Gold's owns the integration layer and data model
  • Best-in-class selection — each component chosen for category strength
  • Corporate as super-franchisee — same platform, no parallel systems
  • Vendor replaceable — any component can be swapped without rebuilding
  • Asset-light delivery — vendor-managed infrastructure, Gold's manages relationships
  • Future-ready architecture — integration layer designed so Gold's can selectively replace vendor components with custom-built solutions as capabilities mature

  • KPI tree and success metrics
