## 1. Strategic Context & Introduction

### 1.1 About Gold's Gym

Gold's Gym operates a network of approximately **400 locations across the United States** under a mix of **corporate-owned and franchised** ownership, on an asset-light operating model, with an active franchise-growth agenda. Member base and transaction volumes are significant and will be shared with short-listed vendors. [Gold's to confirm — member base, annual transaction volume, peak concurrent load.]

### 1.2 The Initiative — One Platform, One Experience, One Data Layer

Gold's is unifying a fragmented technology environment into a single, coherent platform. Rather than adopting one vendor's end-to-end suite, Gold's is **assembling** a platform: **Gold's owns the data layer and the member experience (our website and app), and best-in-class vendors integrate into a Gold's-controlled integration layer via APIs.**

The MMS is the operational system of record for member data, billing, check-in, and collections — the foundational component of this platform and the subject of this RFP.

### 1.3 Operating Principles

These principles define how Gold's works with technology partners and frame the requirements that follow:

- **"We assemble the platform — we do not rent it."**
- **"All member data belongs to Gold's, not the vendor."**
- **"Vendors operate within our system, not outside of it."**

### 1.4 Why Now

Modernizing the MMS unblocks the rest of the platform: a unified member view across locations, real-time data into our analytics layer, a consistent member experience across web and app, and accurate franchise-level reporting.

## 2. Purpose & Scope

### 2.1 In Scope

A Member Management System with a tightly-coupled retail POS, covering:

- Member lifecycle (join, onboarding, freeze, cancel, win-back)
- Membership plans and pricing configuration
- Billing and collections (ACH, card, dunning, chargebacks, settlement)
- Check-in and gate/kiosk integration
- Travel pass / multi-location usage tracking
- Retail POS with member-account linkage
- Operational reporting and franchisee scorecards
- Transactional member communications (receipts, billing notices, freeze confirmations)

### 2.2 Also Evaluated (Capability and Openness)

Gold's will evaluate vendors' capabilities — and openness to third-party alternatives — in **CRM** (§5.9), **member mobile app** (§5.10), and **analytics & data access** (§6.3). Gold's is not necessarily procuring these from the MMS vendor; the goal is to understand whether they are native, optional, or replaceable.

### 2.3 Out of Scope (Adjacent Procurements)

- CRM (incumbent: HubSpot; future state TBD)
- Member-facing mobile app (incumbent: Motion Vibe)
- Unified data platform (Databricks / Snowflake / Microsoft Fabric — selection pending)
- Marketing automation and campaign management

### 2.4 Integration Dependencies

The MMS must integrate cleanly with — but not own — the systems above plus the payment processor, gate-control hardware, and Gold's data warehouse. Full inventory in Appendix B.

## 4. Mandatory Requirements (Threshold)

The following are mandatory. For each, the vendor must **confirm compliance and describe how** (see the confirmation checklist in Appendix C). These reflect the operating principles in §1.3 and are central to evaluation.

- **4.1 Full data ownership.** Gold's owns all member data, schema, derived data, and outputs; the vendor holds usage rights for service delivery only. Must be contractual.
- **4.2 Real-time API access.** REST APIs and webhooks supporting event-driven, real-time synchronization. Batch-only architectures do not meet this requirement.
- **4.3 Multi-location franchise architecture.** Built for franchise/multi-location from the ground up — not a single-club product with multi-location added on.
- **4.4 Documented data export and migration.** Full historical export in standard formats (CSV, JSON, Parquet) on demand, and contractual cooperation with any future migration.
- **4.5 U.S. market support.** Coverage, regulatory familiarity, and support hours aligned to U.S. operations.
- **4.6 POS / retail capability.** Retail POS integrated into or tightly coupled with the MMS, so members and retail transactions share one account of truth.
- **4.7 Open ecosystem and headless compatibility.** The platform operates as an open platform Gold's builds on top of, not a closed suite requiring full-suite adoption for full value. APIs are first-class; the vendor UI is optional; Gold's can integrate third-party CRM, app, marketing, analytics, and PT tools without commercial penalty, support degradation, or roadmap deprioritization. The vendor should be comfortable serving as the **headless system of record** while Gold's owns workflows and the member experience.

These questions help Gold's understand whether your platform is one Gold's builds on top of, or one that captures data, workflows, and UI inside its own walls. Answer each with **specific numbers, lists, or named examples** — not adjectives.

If yes, describe what is required and name customers operating this way. If no, explain specifically why.
- **API pricing and metering.** Are APIs included in the base subscription or metered/billed separately? Provide per-call cost at scale, rate-limit tiers, and whether higher API throughput requires a different commercial tier.
- **Webhook and event coverage.** Provide the full catalog of webhook events fired today (member, billing, transaction, access, plan-change, communication events). Mark which require polling. State a latency target per event type.
- **Third-party integration policy.** Describe your integrations marketplace and approval model. Which categories of third-party integration are self-service, which require partnership/approval, and which are discouraged or blocked? Provide examples of customers running non-vendor CRM, app, marketing, and analytics tools.

List every commercial or operational difference, including bundle discounts that change economics when customers adopt additional modules.

- **Vendor use of customer data.** Does customer data ever flow into shared analytics, vendor-internal benchmarking, AI/ML training, or product-development datasets? Under what terms? Can the customer opt out completely?
- **UI replaceability.** For each major member-facing surface (join, account, billing, freeze, cancel, check-in confirmation), can your UI be fully replaced by a Gold's-built UI calling your APIs? Where it cannot, explain the constraint.
- **Exit.** Describe what a Gold's exit looks like operationally — data formats provided, time to extract, your cooperation during transition, and what customer-owned customizations and integrations stay vs. leave. Has any customer of comparable scale exited in the last 24 months?

## 13. Evaluation Criteria & Methodology

Gold's will evaluate proposals against the following factors. The weighting deliberately favors integration and data control over feature breadth — fit into Gold's architecture matters more than the size of the feature checklist. **Detailed scoring weights are maintained internally and are not published in this RFP.**

Evaluation factors:

- **API & Integration** — API maturity and coverage, webhooks/event coverage, real-time sync, sandbox, documentation, and ecosystem openness (headless / system-of-record).
- **Data Ownership & Portability** — contractual data ownership, export formats, migration tooling and commitment, and vendor-lock-in risk.
- **Franchise Architecture** — native multi-tenant design, corporate vs. franchisee controls, configuration hierarchy, and scale/onboarding evidence.
- **Core Features** — member lifecycle, plans/pricing, billing & collections, check-in, travel pass, POS, reporting, and communications.
- **Commercial Terms** — pricing transparency, term flexibility, incentives, volume discounts, tech-fee revenue-share, and API economics.
- **Reliability & SLA** — uptime, response/resolution by severity, DR, and track record at scale.
- **Innovation & Roadmap** — AI/ML, mobile-first investment, product velocity, and platform-vs-suite ecosystem strategy.

Evaluation process: written response review → finalist demonstrations (§14) → reference checks (§12.3) → selection. Demonstrations and reference checks feed evidence into the factors above.
