01 THE THREE MODELS

How should Gold's Gym acquire its next-generation technology platform? Three fundamental approaches exist, each with real precedent in the fitness franchise industry.

BUILD
  • What: Custom-build a proprietary platform tailored to Gold's exact specifications
  • Timeline: Traditionally 18-36 months to MVP (compressing with AI-accelerated development)
  • Cost: $2-5M+ initial development, $1-2M+ annual maintenance (traditional estimates trending downward)
  • Team: Traditionally 15-25+ engineers, product managers, QA, DevOps (declining with AI tooling)
  • Control: Complete ownership of code, roadmap, and IP
  • Risk: Highest — talent acquisition, scope creep, maintenance burden
  • Who does it: Very few — Peloton (member app), some boutique chains with VC backing
  • AI factor: AI-assisted development is compressing traditional timelines and reducing required team sizes — a trend accelerating monthly
Historically prohibitive — but AI-accelerated development is rapidly changing this equation. Worth serious re-evaluation in Phase 1.
ASSEMBLE
  • What: Select best-in-class vendors per category, integrate via owned API layer
  • Timeline: 8-12 months from vendor selection to pilot
  • Cost: $200-500K integration + vendor SaaS fees
  • Team: 3-5 people (integration architect, project manager, vendor managers)
  • Control: Own data model and integration layer; swap vendors as needed
  • Risk: Moderate — integration complexity, vendor coordination
  • Who does it: Xponential (Mariana Tek + integrations), Crunch (ABC + custom layers), Lift Brands (multi-vendor stack), MASS Fitness
Asset-light by design. Matches Gold's DNA. How every successful fitness franchisor operates.
BUY
  • What: Select a single vendor to provide all functionality as a unified suite
  • Timeline: 6-9 months deployment
  • Cost: Per-location SaaS fees, lower upfront investment
  • Team: 1-2 vendor relationship managers
  • Control: Minimal — locked into vendor's roadmap and release cycle
  • Risk: High — vendor dependency, all eggs in one basket
  • Who does it: Smaller franchise brands with simple requirements
No single vendor covers all Gold's requirements. Current ABC/Motionsoft scores (2.0-2.3/5) prove single-vendor gaps.
THE LANDSCAPE IS SHIFTING

AI is fundamentally changing the Build calculus. What traditionally required 15-25 engineers and 18+ months can increasingly be accomplished with smaller teams in shorter timeframes. The Assemble model is the right starting point today, but the integration layer and data ownership it establishes are also the exact foundation needed if Gold's evolves toward a Build approach. Phase 1 strategy work will evaluate this trajectory with current capabilities.

ASSET-LIGHT BY DESIGN

The Assemble model requires only 3-5 additional team members — not the 15-25+ headcount of a traditional Build approach. Gold's manages vendor relationships and owns the integration strategy, while vendors manage their own infrastructure, support, and product development. This is the same model that Xponential Fitness, Lift Brands, and MASS Fitness use to operate technology platforms across hundreds of locations. Owning the integration layer also positions Gold's to selectively build custom components where vendors fall short — a capability that becomes increasingly viable as AI-driven development matures.

03 FRANCHISOR SERVICES TO CONSIDER

Gold's currently collects $0 in technology fees or rebates from franchisees. Competitors like Crunch charge $5 per online join, percentage of billing as rebate, and tech fees built into franchise agreements. This represents a significant untapped revenue stream that can fund the entire support infrastructure.
