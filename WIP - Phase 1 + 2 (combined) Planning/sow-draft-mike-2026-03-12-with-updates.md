4.  [[Deliverables/Work Product]{.underline}. The defined Work Product
    is for the Client The defined Work Product for this SOW is to
    support the Client during Phase 1 and Phase 2 of the Engagement,
    focused on strategic decision making, architecture design, vendor
    evaluation, and implementation planning. The primary deliverables
    shall include the Gold's Gym Strategy & Playbook and the
    Architecture Blueprint & Implementation Plan (collectively, the
    "Presentation"). The Presentation shall include the following
    findings, recommendations, and supporting materials:]{.mark}

    a.  [Technology Strategy]{.mark}
        - System of One platform strategy with trade-off analysis (unified vs. best-of-breed vs. hybrid)
        - Strategic alignment to Gold's Strong Model and five strategic pillars
        - Technology Decision Framework with evaluation filters aligned to business objectives

    b.  [Architectural Guardrails and System of One Framework]{.mark}
        - Non-negotiable technology principles (API-first, multi-tenant, franchise-scalable, data-unified)
        - Platform approach decision with rationale and Phase 0 scoring data
        - Systems retained vs. replaced vs. net-new determination

    c.  [Franchise Technology Framework]{.mark}
        - Required vs. optional systems framework for franchise operators
        - Transition requirements for current ABC operators (80%+ of network)
        - Compliance and enforcement mechanisms for franchise technology standards

    d.  [Data Strategy, AI Plan and Canonical Data Model]{.mark}
        - Unified data ownership model (brand vs. franchisee) and single member profile strategy
        - Canonical entity model (Member, Location, Transaction, Billing, Check-In, Class/Session)
        - AI enablement points including churn prediction, lead scoring, dynamic pricing, and automated royalty calculation

    e.  [Target System Architecture and Integration Topology]{.mark}
        - C4-style context and container diagrams (L1/L2) for target-state architecture
        - Integration topology map with API strategy (REST, event-driven, batch)
        - Current-to-target gap mapping with migration complexity assessment

    f.  [Vendor Evaluation and Recommendation Summary]{.mark}
        - Vendor shortlist evaluation against 150+ requirements with weighted comparison matrix
        - TCO modeling (3-year and 5-year) by vendor scenario
        - Directional recommendation with trade-offs and Phase 3 RFP prerequisites

    g.  [Implementation Wave Plan, Timeline and Resources]{.mark}
        - Phased implementation roadmap with wave definitions, sequencing, and dependencies
        - Corporate and franchise migration strategies with cutover criteria and rollback plans
        - High-level resource requirements and budget envelopes by wave

    h.  [Governance Model]{.mark}
        - Steering committee structure and cadence
        - Decision rights matrix (RACI) covering executive, technology, franchise policy, and operational decisions
        - Change management process and technology governance cadence

    i.  [Technology Fee Model Analysis]{.mark}
        - Competitive analysis of franchise technology fee structures (Crunch, Planet Fitness, Xponential)
        - Revenue modeling scenarios (per-join, per-member-per-month, billing rebate, hybrid)
        - Opportunity sizing and recommended model with projected revenue

    j.  [Identity & Permissions Architecture]{.mark}
        - Unified member ID across franchise network enabling reciprocity and travel pass tracking
        - Role-based access control (RBAC) with permission hierarchy (brand, franchise group, location, role)
        - Single sign-on strategy for member and staff identity

    k.  [Reporting & Analytics Strategy]{.mark}
        - Dashboard framework (executive, franchise group, location) with KPI alignment
        - BI tool strategy and recommendation
        - Conceptual dashboard wireframes and real-time vs. batch reporting by use case

    l.  [Phase 3 Readiness & Handoff Documentation]{.mark}
        - Phase 3 (RFPs & Vendor Selection) scope outline and prerequisites
        - Open items carried forward and recommended Phase 3 timeline
        - Risk register with severity ratings and mitigation strategies

**[APPENDIX C]{.mark}**

**Engagement Phases**

This is a high-level breakdown, with estimate, of the Engagement phases
to fully develop and implement the Client Technology Stack. These
timelines, work and Work Product deliverables are all good faith
estimates and not final commitments and only used for general planning &
discussion. The Phase 0 Audit will help the Company refine the timeline,
work and Work Product into a more formal work plan. SOW No. 02 governs
only Services to be provided under some of Phase 1 and Phase 2.

**Phase 0 --- Audit & Discovery (4--6 weeks) + 2 Week Contingency**\
Work: knowledge transfer, stakeholder interviews, surveys, current-state
stack & data review, pain-point mapping, risk & dependency register.\
Work Product Deliverables: Current-State Map, Gap/Risk Report,
Leadership Decision List.

**Phase 1 --- Decisions & Strategy (4--6 weeks)**\
Work: architectural guardrails, "system of one" principles, data-first &
AI-readiness strategy, governance, franchise policies (required vs.
optional), KPI tree.\
Deliverables: Gold's Gym Strategy & Playbook (signed off).

**Phase 2 --- Architecture & Blueprint (4--6 weeks)**\
Work: target architecture, data model, integration topology,
identity/permissions, reporting layer, AI enablement points,
implementation wave plan.\
Deliverables: Architecture Blueprint & Implementation Plan.

**Phase 3 --- Resourcing, RFPs & Vendor Selection (6-8 weeks)**\
Work: RFPs, scorecards, bake-offs/POCs, commercials, SLAs, timeline &
responsibility matrix (RACI), budget envelope.\
Deliverables: Vendor Selections + Resource Plan + Master Timeline.

**Phase 4 --- Implementation Oversight (initial 16--24 weeks)**\
Work: PMO/advisory, backlog hygiene, cutover/runbooks, data migration
oversight, security & compliance checks, reporting pack stand-up.\
Deliverables: Systems live for corporate clubs; onboarding-ready.

**Phase 5 --- Beta with Corporate Clubs (6 - 12 weeks)**\
Work: pilot configuration, SOP draft/iterate, training, NPS/operational
metrics, fix/finish list, acceptance criteria validation.\
Deliverables: Pilot Report, Finalized SOPs, Go/No-Go.

**Phase 6 --- Franchisee Rollout & Onboarding (12--24 weeks)**\
Work: rollout playbook, enablement/training content, tiered support,
data checks, required vs. opt-in system packaging/pricing guidance,
governance cadence.\
Deliverables: Rollout Factory + Training Assets + Adoption Dashboards.

**Phase 7 --- Ongoing Advisory & Governance (Monthly)**\
Work: steering committee, roadmap refresh, vendor performance
management, data/AI use-case incubation, franchise feedback loop.
Deliverables: Monthly scorecards & roadmap updates.
