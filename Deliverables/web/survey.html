Summary: Franchisees are nearly unanimous — billing infrastructure must migrate flawlessly. Across all three priority slots, billing-related functions (EFT drafting, dues accuracy, payment history, high collection rates) appear most frequently. Member check-in and door access is the second most consistent theme, especially for 24/7 locations. Reporting quality and data accuracy rounds out the top three concerns — multiple respondents explicitly called out current inaccuracies in ABC Fitness reports as a pain point they don't want carried forward. A successful migration strategy must de-risk all three areas before any cutover.

Strategic Recommendation

The unified platform should prioritize system integration and data accuracy first (addressing #1 concern), followed by an improved member-facing app (lowest satisfaction score, most friction). A transparent, opt-in benchmarking program would unlock goodwill with the 84% of franchisees conditionally open to sharing data — creating a competitive differentiator for the entire network.
